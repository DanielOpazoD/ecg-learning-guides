## Categorías y explicaciones

### proxLAD

Anterior STE in V1–V4, plus V1 (septum, supplied by S1) and I and aVL (high lateral wall, supplied by D1), with reciprocal STD in III and aVF.

Derivaciones: V1, V2, V3, I, aVL, III

Ecocardiografía: The whole LAD territory: anterior wall, anterior septum and apex akinetic from base to apex, usually with the anterolateral wall.

### distLAD

Anterior STE in V2–V5 but not V1 or aVL (S1 and D1 are spared). If the LAD wraps the apex, II, III and aVF rise too, and aVL is not depressed.

Derivaciones: V2, V3, V4, V5, aVL, II

Ecocardiografía: Mid and apical anterior wall, apical septum and apex abnormal, with the basal segments spared.

### highLat

STE in I and aVL (often V2) with reciprocal STD in III, while V3–V5 stay relatively flat: the high lateral wall.

Derivaciones: I, aVL, V2, III

Ecocardiografía: Basal and mid anterolateral wall (and the basal anterior wall) hypokinetic; septum, apex and inferior wall normal.

### proxRCA

Inferior STE with III > II, reciprocal STD in aVL and I, and STE in V1 (and V4R) from the right ventricle.

Derivaciones: III, II, aVL, I, V1

Ecocardiografía: Inferior wall and inferior septum akinetic plus an abnormal RV free wall with reduced TAPSE.

### infRCA

Inferior STE with III > II and STD in aVL, often with posterior STD in V1–V3, but V1 is not elevated: the RV branch is spared.

Derivaciones: II, III, aVF, aVL, V1, V2

Ecocardiografía: Inferior and inferoseptal walls abnormal, with a normal RV.

### LCx

STD maximal in V1–V3 (posterior wall seen from the front) with mild STE in I, aVL and V5–V6; the inferior leads change little.

Derivaciones: V1, V2, V3, I, V6

Ecocardiografía: Lateral and inferolateral walls abnormal; the inferior wall and septum are spared.

### domLCx

Inferior STE with II ≥ III and no STD in lead I (the vector points left), plus posterior STD in V1–V3 and lateral STE in V5–V6.

Derivaciones: II, III, I, V2, V6

Ecocardiografía: Inferior and inferolateral walls abnormal, extending to the lateral wall; RV normal.

### LM

Extensive STE in I, aVL and V2–V6 with reciprocal inferior STD: the LAD and circumflex territories together.

Derivaciones: I, aVL, V2, V4, V6, III

Ecocardiografía: Most of the LV abnormal (anterior, septal, lateral and inferolateral), with only the inferior wall spared.

### none

No STE in a coronary territory: either a normal ECG, or diffuse STD (maximal in V4–V6) with STE in aVR from subendocardial ischaemia.

Derivaciones: aVR, V5, V6

Ecocardiografía: No regional abnormality: normal, or mild global hypokinesis that follows no coronary distribution.
