# 5. Time course and reperfusion

Hyperacute, acute, evolving, reperfused, re-occluded

Duración indicada: 20 min

## Objetivos

- Date an occlusion from the ECG
- Recognise reperfusion T waves, including posterior
- Spot pseudo-normalisation from re-occlusion

## 1. The ECG is a clock

Tipo: read · Identificador: m5s1

Occlusion brings hyperacute T waves within minutes, then ST elevation, then over hours Q waves (loss of R) and T inversion as the ST settles.
Reperfusion is different: the ST resolves quickly and the T waves invert in the leads that were elevated (Wellens in the LAD). Seen from the front, posterior reperfusion gives tall T waves in V1–V3. If reperfusion T waves turn upright again while pain returns, the artery has re-occluded. The echo stays abnormal for days because reperfused muscle is stunned.

Escenario: wellensB

## 6. Check: time course

Tipo: check · Identificador: m5s6


### Wellens T waves mean the LAD:

1. Occluded and reperfused, leaving a critical lesion that can re-occlude
2. Is occluded right now
3. Was never involved
4. Is filled by collaterals

**Respuesta del original:** Occluded and reperfused, leaving a critical lesion that can re-occlude

**Explicación:** Urgent angiography; stress testing can provoke re-occlusion.

### Tall upright T waves in V1–V3 after a circumflex occlusion settles suggest:

1. Posterior reperfusion
2. A new anterior OMI
3. Hyperkalaemia
4. LVH

**Respuesta del original:** Posterior reperfusion

**Explicación:** Posterior T inversion seen from the front.

### Wellens T waves become upright again as pain returns. This is:

1. Pseudo-normalisation from re-occlusion
2. Healing
3. Reperfusion
4. A benign variant

**Respuesta del original:** Pseudo-normalisation from re-occlusion

**Explicación:** Treat it as an occlusion: the next ECG may show STE.

### After successful reperfusion, regional wall motion:

1. Can stay abnormal for days (stunning) before recovering
2. Normalises immediately
3. Becomes hyperkinetic
4. Is unaffected because the artery is open

**Respuesta del original:** Can stay abnormal for days (stunning) before recovering

**Explicación:** So an echo after reperfusion still shows the territory.
