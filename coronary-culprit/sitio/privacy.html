Privacy

Coronary Culprit is a free teaching tool by Dr Cliff Reid, on the web at cliffreid.com/culprit and as an iPhone app. Last updated 28 October 2026.

The short version

  • No account, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Your progress (drill mastery, case scores, course steps) is saved on your own device and never sent anywhere.
  • The tool counts how it is used, anonymously, so it can be improved.
  • If you send feedback, what you write is stored so it can be reviewed. An email address is optional and used only to reply to you.

Anonymous usage counts

When you open the tool and use its parts, it sends a short record of what happened: for example a visit, which mode you opened (Explore, Drill, Cases, Course, Challenge), a case you finished and its score, or a click through to cliffreid.com. Each record carries the tool's version and a random code made on your device, so repeat visits can be told apart from new ones. The code is not linked to your name, your device's identity or anything else about you.

To stop abuse, the server also keeps a one-way scrambled form (a hash) of the internet address the record came from. The address itself is never stored, and the hash cannot be turned back into it.

Feedback

The “Seen an error?” form sends what you type, where you were in the tool and the state of the simulation at the time (for example the artery and occlusion shown), so the report can be checked against the evidence. You can choose to add a reference, say that you have specialist expertise, or give an email address; the email address is used only to reply to you, and never shared. Please do not include anything that could identify a patient.

Where it is kept

Usage counts and feedback are stored in a database run on Cloudflare, which hosts cliffreid.com. They are used only by Dr Cliff Reid to improve the tool, and are never sold or shared.

Links out

Links to cliffreid.com and the Resuscitation Wizardry course open in your web browser, where those sites' own terms apply.

Your choices

You can clear the saved progress and the random code by deleting the app, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. To have feedback you sent removed, send a note through the feedback form saying so, with enough detail to find it.

Not medical advice

Coronary Culprit is an idealised teaching model for clinicians. It does not diagnose or advise on the care of any patient.
