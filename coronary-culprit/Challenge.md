# Challenge — desafíos

Cada ronda genera diez preguntas. Hay cinco tipos:

- **culprit**: ['Where is the occlusion?', 'Read the ECG and name the culprit artery.']
- **ecg**: ['Which ECG does this occlusion produce?', 'The thrombus is shown on the heart. Pick the matching 12-lead.']
- **pattern**: ['Name this pattern', 'Identify the electrocardiographic sign.']
- **predict**: ['Predict the 12-lead', 'The thrombus is shown on the heart. Mark every lead ▲ ST elevation, ▼ ST depression or — no change, then check.']
- **place**: ['Place the clot', 'Read the ECG, then click the artery where you think the occlusion is. Click again to move it.']
