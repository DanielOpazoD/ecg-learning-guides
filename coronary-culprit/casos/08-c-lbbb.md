# LBBB with anterior OMI

75-year-old man with known LBBB

45 minutes of crushing chest pain

HR 104 · BP 102/64 · SpO₂ 93% · RR 24

## Evolución del dolor

- Minuto 0: Ongoing, 9/10

## Explicación del caso

In LBBB the ST segment normally runs opposite the QRS and stays proportional to it. Here the discordant STE in V1–V3 exceeds 25% of the S wave and there is concordant STE laterally: Smith-modified Sgarbossa positive. Activate.

Los ECG seriados y las acciones que hacen avanzar el reloj se conservan en la aplicación interactiva.
