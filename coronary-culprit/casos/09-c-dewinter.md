# de Winter T waves

50-year-old man

30 minutes of crushing central chest pain

HR 88 · BP 146/92 · SpO₂ 97% · RR 18

## Evolución del dolor

- Minuto 0: Ongoing, 8/10

## Explicación del caso

Upsloping J-point depression with tall, symmetric precordial T waves and slight STE in aVR: de Winter T waves, a STEMI equivalent of proximal LAD occlusion. Activate on the first ECG.
