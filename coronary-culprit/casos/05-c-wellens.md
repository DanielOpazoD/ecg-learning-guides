# Wellens syndrome with re-occlusion

47-year-old man

Three hours of chest pain earlier today, now pain-free

HR 70 · BP 138/84 · SpO₂ 98% · RR 14

## Evolución del dolor

- Minuto 0: Pain-free
- Minuto 45: Pain has returned, 8/10, sweaty

## Explicación del caso

Deep symmetric T inversion in V1–V4 in a pain-free patient is Wellens B: the LAD occluded, then reperfused, and the critical lesion is still there. At 45 minutes it re-occluded: the T waves "pseudo-normalised" (upright again) before STE appeared. Wellens needs urgent angiography; activating from the first ECG would have prevented the re-occlusion infarct.

Los ECG seriados y las acciones que hacen avanzar el reloj se conservan en la aplicación interactiva.
