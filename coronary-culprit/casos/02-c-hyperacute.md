# Hyperacute T waves before STE

52-year-old man

Two hours of intermittent chest pressure, constant and worse for the last 10 minutes

HR 92 · BP 148/90 · SpO₂ 97% · RR 18

## Evolución del dolor

- Minuto 0: Ongoing, 7/10

## Explicación del caso

The arrival ECG showed hyperacute T waves in V2–V4: broad, bulky and out of proportion to the QRS, with little STE yet. STEMI criteria were met only on the repeat. An expert reader activates on the first tracing; at worst, the 15-minute repeat makes it obvious.
