# Takotsubo syndrome

68-year-old woman

Chest pain and breathlessness on the morning of her husband’s funeral

HR 98 · BP 112/70 · SpO₂ 95% · RR 22

## Evolución del dolor

- Minuto 0: Ongoing, 6/10

## Explicación del caso

Anterior and inferior STE, STD in aVR and no STE in V1, with apical ballooning and a hyperkinetic base on echo: the picture favours Takotsubo. But the ECG cannot exclude LAD occlusion, so angiography is the right call. It showed unobstructed coronaries.

Los ECG seriados y las acciones que hacen avanzar el reloj se conservan en la aplicación interactiva.
