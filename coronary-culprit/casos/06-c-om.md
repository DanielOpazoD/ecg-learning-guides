# ECG-silent obtuse marginal occlusion

70-year-old woman

90 minutes of persistent central chest pressure, unrelieved by GTN

HR 88 · BP 144/86 · SpO₂ 96% · RR 18

## Evolución del dolor

- Minuto 0: Ongoing, 7/10, refractory to GTN

## Explicación del caso

The ECGs barely moved: obtuse marginal territory faces away from the standard leads. Refractory ischaemic pain plus a new lateral wall motion abnormality on bedside echo is enough to activate. Waiting for troponin, or for STE that may never appear, costs myocardium.

Los ECG seriados y las acciones que hacen avanzar el reloj se conservan en la aplicación interactiva.
