# Occlusion after arrival: the value of serial ECGs

61-year-old man

20 minutes of epigastric discomfort he thinks is indigestion

HR 78 · BP 152/88 · SpO₂ 98% · RR 16

## Evolución del dolor

- Minuto 0: Mild, 3/10
- Minuto 12: Suddenly worse, 8/10, now clammy and nauseated

## Explicación del caso

The arrival ECG was normal because the artery had not yet occluded. At 12 minutes the pain escalated; the 15-minute ECG showed hyperacute inferior T waves with T-wave inversion in aVL, and by 30 minutes there was inferior STE. A normal first ECG never excludes OMI: repeat it every 15–30 minutes while symptoms continue, and immediately when they change.

Los ECG seriados y las acciones que hacen avanzar el reloj se conservan en la aplicación interactiva.
