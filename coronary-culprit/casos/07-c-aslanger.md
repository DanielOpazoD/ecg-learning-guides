# Aslanger pattern

66-year-old woman with diabetes

Two hours of malaise, breathlessness and vague chest heaviness

HR 96 · BP 118/72 · SpO₂ 94% · RR 22

## Evolución del dolor

- Minuto 0: Ongoing, 5/10

## Explicación del caso

STE only in III, STD in V4–V6 but not V2, and V1 higher than V2: the Aslanger pattern of inferior OMI with multivessel disease. It does not meet STEMI criteria. Activate on the first ECG.

Los ECG seriados y las acciones que hacen avanzar el reloj se conservan en la aplicación interactiva.
