# Benign early repolarisation

34-year-old man, a keen runner

Brief twinges of left chest discomfort after the gym, now well

HR 56 · BP 124/72 · SpO₂ 99% · RR 14

## Evolución del dolor

- Minuto 0: Pain-free

## Explicación del caso

STE in V2–V4 with a notched J point, tall but proportionate T waves and preserved S waves; serial ECGs are identical and the echo is normal. Benign early repolarisation, no activation. Compare it with the hyperacute-T case: there the T waves were bulky relative to the QRS and changed within 15 minutes.

Los ECG seriados y las acciones que hacen avanzar el reloj se conservan en la aplicación interactiva.
