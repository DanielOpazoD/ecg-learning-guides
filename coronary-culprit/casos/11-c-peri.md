# Acute pericarditis

28-year-old man

Two days of sharp chest pain, worse lying flat and better sitting forward, after a viral illness

HR 104 · BP 128/76 · SpO₂ 98% · RR 18 · T 38.1 °C

## Evolución del dolor

- Minuto 0: Sharp, pleuritic, 5/10

## Explicación del caso

Diffuse concave STE (II > III), PR depression and STD only in aVR and V1, with no STD in aVL. Echo shows a small effusion and normal wall motion. Serial ECGs do not evolve. This is pericarditis: activating the cath lab would be a false alarm.
