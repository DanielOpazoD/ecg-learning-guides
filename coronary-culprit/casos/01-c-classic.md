# Inferior STEMI with RV involvement

58-year-old man

Crushing chest pain for 70 minutes, vomiting, clammy

HR 50 · BP 88/56 · SpO₂ 95% · RR 20

## Evolución del dolor

- Minuto 0: Ongoing, 9/10

## Explicación del caso

Inferior STE with III > II, reciprocal STD in aVL and I, STE in V1 with V4R elevation: proximal RCA occlusion with RV infarction. Bradycardia and hypotension fit, so avoid nitrates and give fluid cautiously. This one should be activated on the first ECG.

Los ECG seriados y las acciones que hacen avanzar el reloj se conservan en la aplicación interactiva.
