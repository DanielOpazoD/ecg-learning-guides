# Proximal RCA

With RV infarction

Classic territories

Proximal RCA occlusion takes the RV branch with it. There is inferior STE with III > II, and reciprocal STD in aVL and I. V1 is elevated (from the RV) while V2 is depressed (posterior wall), and V4R shows STE. Expect bradycardia or AV block, and hypotension if nitrates are given.

Patrón: Right ventricular infarction

## ECG

Inferior STE with III > II, reciprocal STD in aVL and I, and STE in V1 (and V4R) from the right ventricle.

## Ecocardiografía

Inferior wall and inferior septum akinetic plus an abnormal RV free wall with reduced TAPSE.
