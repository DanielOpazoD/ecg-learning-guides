# Pericarditis

Diffuse STE, PR depression

OMI mimics

STE is diffuse and concave, spanning inferior and lateral territories at once, with II > III. PR depression (and PR elevation in aVR) is a clue. The decisive point is what is missing: no reciprocal STD except in aVR and V1, and in particular no STD in aVL, which accompanies nearly every inferior OMI. Echo shows normal wall motion, sometimes with an effusion.

Patrón: Acute pericarditis
