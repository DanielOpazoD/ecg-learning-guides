# Hyperacute T waves

Minutes after LAD occlusion

Subtle & advanced OMI

In the first minutes the T waves become broad-based, bulky and symmetric before the ST segment lifts much. Their area grows relative to the QRS, and proportion is the key. A T wave this large in V2–V4 is OMI until proven otherwise. Repeat the ECG every 15–30 minutes.

Patrón: Hyperacute T waves

## ECG

Anterior STE in V1–V4, plus V1 (septum, supplied by S1) and I and aVL (high lateral wall, supplied by D1), with reciprocal STD in III and aVF.

## Ecocardiografía

The whole LAD territory: anterior wall, anterior septum and apex akinetic from base to apex, usually with the anterolateral wall.
