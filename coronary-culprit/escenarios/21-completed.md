# Completed anterior MI

Late presentation

Reperfusion, evolution & subendocardial

Hours to days after occlusion, Q waves appear as the R waves are lost, the ST elevation subsides and the T waves invert. Q waves with residual STE suggest a completed infarct or an aneurysm. The size of the T wave relative to the QRS helps date the infarct.

Patrón: Completed infarction (Q waves, T inversion)

## ECG

Anterior STE in V1–V4, plus V1 (septum, supplied by S1) and I and aVL (high lateral wall, supplied by D1), with reciprocal STD in III and aVF.

## Ecocardiografía

The whole LAD territory: anterior wall, anterior septum and apex akinetic from base to apex, usually with the anterolateral wall.
