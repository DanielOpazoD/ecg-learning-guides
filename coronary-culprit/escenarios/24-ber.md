# Early repolarisation

J-point notch, V2–V4

OMI mimics

STE is maximal in V2–V4 and concave, with a notched or slurred J point, large QRS voltages and tall T waves that stay in proportion to the QRS. S waves in V2–V3 are preserved and there is no reciprocal STD. Subtle anterior OMI is the mimic of the mimic: bulky T waves, a lower R wave in V4, a longer QTc or any terminal QRS distortion should make you think again.
