# Wellens B

Later reperfusion

Reperfusion, evolution & subendocardial

Hours later the reperfusion T waves deepen into symmetric inversion across V1–V4 (Wellens B). This means reperfusion, not ongoing occlusion, but the culprit lesion remains. If the T waves "pseudo-normalise" with recurrent pain, the artery has re-occluded.

Patrón: Wellens pattern B (deep T inversion)

## ECG

Anterior STE in V2–V5 but not V1 or aVL (S1 and D1 are spared). If the LAD wraps the apex, II, III and aVF rise too, and aVL is not depressed.

## Ecocardiografía

Mid and apical anterior wall, apical septum and apex abnormal, with the basal segments spared.
