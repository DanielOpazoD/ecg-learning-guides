# South African flag

First diagonal

Subtle & advanced OMI

High lateral OMI from the first diagonal gives STE in I, aVL and V2 and STD in III, with V3–V5 relatively spared. On the standard 3×4 layout this resembles the South African flag. The "two contiguous leads" rule is only barely met, so this occlusion is easily missed.

Patrón: South African flag sign

## ECG

STE in I and aVL (often V2) with reciprocal STD in III, while V3–V5 stay relatively flat: the high lateral wall.

## Ecocardiografía

Basal and mid anterolateral wall (and the basal anterior wall) hypokinetic; septum, apex and inferior wall normal.
