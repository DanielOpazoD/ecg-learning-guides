# Posterior reperfusion

Tall T waves V1–V3

Reperfusion, evolution & subendocardial

Reperfusion of a posterior (circumflex) occlusion inverts the T waves over the posterior wall. Seen from the front, that inversion becomes tall, broad, upright T waves in V1–V3. It is easily dismissed as normal.

Patrón: Posterior reperfusion T waves

## ECG

STD maximal in V1–V3 (posterior wall seen from the front) with mild STE in I, aVL and V5–V6; the inferior leads change little.

## Ecocardiografía

Lateral and inferolateral walls abnormal; the inferior wall and septum are spared.
