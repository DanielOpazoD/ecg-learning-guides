# Circumflex

Posterior OMI

Classic territories

The circumflex supplies the lateral and inferolateral (posterior) walls, which face away from the standard leads. The main clue is STD maximal in V1–V3 with upright T waves: posterior STE seen from the front. V7–V9 show it directly. Many circumflex occlusions do not meet STEMI criteria.

Patrón: Posterior OMI

## ECG

STD maximal in V1–V3 (posterior wall seen from the front) with mild STE in I, aVL and V5–V6; the inferior leads change little.

## Ecocardiografía

Lateral and inferolateral walls abnormal; the inferior wall and septum are spared.
