# LVH with strain

Discordant STE V1–V3

OMI mimics

Deep S waves in V1–V3 carry discordant STE, and tall lateral R waves carry strain STD with asymmetric T inversion. The STE is proportional to the S wave, much as in LBBB, so judge it by ratio rather than millimetres. Echo shows thick walls, a small cavity and normal wall motion.
