# Hyperkalaemia

Peaked, narrow T waves

OMI mimics

T waves are tall, narrow-based and symmetrically peaked, P waves are flat, the PR is long and the QRS wide, and V1–V2 can show pseudo-STE. Hyperacute T waves of OMI are broad-based and bulky, follow a coronary territory and come with a normal QRS. Echo is normal.
