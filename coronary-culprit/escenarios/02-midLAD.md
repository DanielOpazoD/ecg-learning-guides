# Mid LAD

After D1, no wrap

Classic territories

After D1 the high lateral wall is spared. Expect STE in V2–V5 without elevation in I or aVL, and without inferior reciprocal STD. If the LAD does not wrap the apex, the inferior leads stay quiet.

## ECG

Anterior STE in V2–V5 but not V1 or aVL (S1 and D1 are spared). If the LAD wraps the apex, II, III and aVF rise too, and aVL is not depressed.

## Ecocardiografía

Mid and apical anterior wall, apical septum and apex abnormal, with the basal segments spared.
