# Distal RCA

Inferoposterior

Classic territories

Beyond the RV branch the RV is spared, so V1 joins V2–V3 in posterior STD. Inferior STE with III > II, reciprocal aVL depression, and STE in V7–V9 from the posterolateral branch.

## ECG

Inferior STE with III > II and STD in aVL, often with posterior STD in V1–V3, but V1 is not elevated: the RV branch is spared.

## Ecocardiografía

Inferior and inferoseptal walls abnormal, with a normal RV.
