# Dominant circumflex

Left-dominant system

Classic territories

In a left-dominant system (about 1 in 10 people) the circumflex gives off the PDA. Expect inferior STE with II ≥ III, STE in I and V5–V6, and posterior STD in V1–V3. The RV is not involved because the RCA is small.

## ECG

Inferior STE with II ≥ III and no STD in lead I (the vector points left), plus posterior STD in V1–V3 and lateral STE in V5–V6.

## Ecocardiografía

Inferior and inferolateral walls abnormal, extending to the lateral wall; RV normal.
