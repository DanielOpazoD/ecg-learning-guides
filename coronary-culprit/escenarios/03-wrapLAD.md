# Wraparound LAD

Distal, wraps apex

Classic territories

A "type III" LAD wraps round the apex to supply the inferior apex. Distal occlusion gives STE in V3–V6 and in II, III and aVF. aVL is not depressed, because no high lateral injury opposes the inferior vector. Do not mistake it for pericarditis.

Patrón: Anterior + inferior STE (wraparound LAD)

## ECG

Anterior STE in V2–V5 but not V1 or aVL (S1 and D1 are spared). If the LAD wraps the apex, II, III and aVF rise too, and aVL is not depressed.

## Ecocardiografía

Mid and apical anterior wall, apical septum and apex abnormal, with the basal segments spared.
