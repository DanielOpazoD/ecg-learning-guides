# LV aneurysm

Old MI, persistent STE

OMI mimics

An old anterior infarct leaves QS waves in V1–V4 with persistent STE, but the T waves are small. If the T/QRS amplitude ratio is below 0.36 in every lead from V1 to V4, aneurysm is more likely than acute OMI; above that in any lead, suspect new occlusion. Echo shows a thinned, dyskinetic apex, and the 3D heart shows the old LAD scar.
