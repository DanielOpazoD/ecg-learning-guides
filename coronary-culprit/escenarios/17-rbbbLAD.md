# New RBBB + LAD

S1 supplies the bundle

Subtle & advanced OMI

The right bundle is supplied by the first septal perforator. A new RBBB with anterior STE marks proximal LAD occlusion and a large territory at risk. RBBB does not hide STE, so read the ST segments as usual. T inversion in V1–V2 is expected with RBBB.

## ECG

Anterior STE in V1–V4, plus V1 (septum, supplied by S1) and I and aVL (high lateral wall, supplied by D1), with reciprocal STD in III and aVF.

## Ecocardiografía

The whole LAD territory: anterior wall, anterior septum and apex akinetic from base to apex, usually with the anterolateral wall.
