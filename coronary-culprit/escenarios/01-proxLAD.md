# Proximal LAD

Before S1 and D1

Classic territories

Occlusion before the first septal and first diagonal puts the anterior wall, septum and high lateral wall at risk. Look for STE in V1–V4 plus I and aVL, with reciprocal STD in III and aVF. STE in V1 (septum, via S1) and in aVL (high lateral wall, via D1) shows the occlusion is proximal.

## ECG

Anterior STE in V1–V4, plus V1 (septum, supplied by S1) and I and aVL (high lateral wall, supplied by D1), with reciprocal STD in III and aVF.

## Ecocardiografía

The whole LAD territory: anterior wall, anterior septum and apex akinetic from base to apex, usually with the anterolateral wall.
