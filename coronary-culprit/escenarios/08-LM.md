# Left main

Total occlusion

Classic territories

Total left main occlusion infarcts the LAD and circumflex territories together. There is extensive STE in I, aVL and V2–V6 with inferior reciprocal STD, and the patient usually presents in shock or arrest. Contrast this with subtotal left main disease, which gives diffuse STD with STE in aVR.

## ECG

Extensive STE in I, aVL and V2–V6 with reciprocal inferior STD: the LAD and circumflex territories together.

## Ecocardiografía

Most of the LV abnormal (anterior, septal, lateral and inferolateral), with only the inferior wall spared.
