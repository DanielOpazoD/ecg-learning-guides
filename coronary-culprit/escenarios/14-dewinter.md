# de Winter T waves

Proximal LAD equivalent

Subtle & advanced OMI

In about 2% of proximal LAD occlusions the ECG shows upsloping 1–3 mm J-point depression with tall, symmetric precordial T waves instead of STE, often with ~1 mm STE in aVR. It is a STEMI equivalent: activate the cath lab.

Patrón: de Winter T waves

## ECG

Anterior STE in V1–V4, plus V1 (septum, supplied by S1) and I and aVL (high lateral wall, supplied by D1), with reciprocal STD in III and aVF.

## Ecocardiografía

The whole LAD territory: anterior wall, anterior septum and apex akinetic from base to apex, usually with the anterolateral wall.
