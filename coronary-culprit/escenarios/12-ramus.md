# Ramus intermedius

High lateral, subtle

Subtle & advanced OMI

When present (in roughly a fifth of people), the ramus supplies the same high lateral wall as D1 or a high OM. Occlusion gives small changes in I and aVL with inferior reciprocal STD, often under STEMI thresholds. The reciprocal STD in III can be the most obvious sign.

## ECG

STE in I and aVL (often V2) with reciprocal STD in III, while V3–V5 stay relatively flat: the high lateral wall.

## Ecocardiografía

Basal and mid anterolateral wall (and the basal anterior wall) hypokinetic; septum, apex and inferior wall normal.
