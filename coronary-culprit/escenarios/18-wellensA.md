# Wellens A

Early reperfusion

Reperfusion, evolution & subendocardial

After spontaneous reperfusion of a critical LAD lesion, the pain settles and the ST segments resolve. The T waves in V2–V3 then develop a terminal negative deflection (Wellens A). The artery is open but unstable and likely to re-occlude: arrange urgent angiography and avoid stress testing.

Patrón: Wellens pattern A (biphasic T)

## ECG

Anterior STE in V2–V5 but not V1 or aVL (S1 and D1 are spared). If the LAD wraps the apex, II, III and aVF rise too, and aVL is not depressed.

## Ecocardiografía

Mid and apical anterior wall, apical septum and apex abnormal, with the basal segments spared.
