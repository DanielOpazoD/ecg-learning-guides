# LBBB + OMI

Smith-modified Sgarbossa

Subtle & advanced OMI

LBBB normally produces ST/T changes that are discordant to the QRS and proportional to it. OMI appears as concordant STE (here in V5, where the QRS is positive), or as discordant STE that is excessive for the depth of the S wave. The Smith-modified Sgarbossa rule is positive when discordant STE is ≥25% of the preceding S wave, as in V1–V3 here.

Patrón: Smith-modified Sgarbossa positive

## ECG

Anterior STE in V1–V4, plus V1 (septum, supplied by S1) and I and aVL (high lateral wall, supplied by D1), with reciprocal STD in III and aVF.

## Ecocardiografía

The whole LAD territory: anterior wall, anterior septum and apex akinetic from base to apex, usually with the anterolateral wall.
