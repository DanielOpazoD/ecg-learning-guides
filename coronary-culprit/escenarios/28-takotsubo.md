# Takotsubo

Apical ballooning

OMI mimics

Anterior and often inferior STE, STD in aVR and no STE in V1 favour Takotsubo, but the ECG cannot exclude LAD occlusion, so angiography is still needed. The echo is the giveaway: the mid and apical walls stop moving all the way round while the base is hyperkinetic, crossing every coronary territory.

Patrón: Takotsubo syndrome
