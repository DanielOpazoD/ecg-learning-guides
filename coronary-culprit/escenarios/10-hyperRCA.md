# Earliest inferior OMI

Watch aVL

Subtle & advanced OMI

In early inferior OMI there are subtle hyperacute T waves in III and aVF, with T-wave flattening or inversion and slight STD in aVL. The reciprocal change in aVL is often the earliest and most specific sign, and it helps separate OMI from benign variants.

Patrón: Reciprocal change in aVL

## ECG

Inferior STE with III > II and STD in aVL, often with posterior STD in V1–V3, but V1 is not elevated: the RV branch is spared.

## Ecocardiografía

Inferior and inferoseptal walls abnormal, with a normal RV.
