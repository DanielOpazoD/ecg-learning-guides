# Aslanger pattern

Inferior OMI + multivessel

Subtle & advanced OMI

This is inferior OMI (here the PDA) with diffuse subendocardial ischaemia from multivessel disease. Adding the inferior vector to a vector pointing at aVR leaves STE only in III. V4–V6 are depressed while V2 is not, and V1 sits higher than V2. Aslanger and colleagues (2020) described it as a sign of inferior OMI with multivessel disease, and STEMI criteria miss it.

Patrón: Aslanger pattern

## ECG

Inferior STE with III > II and STD in aVL, often with posterior STD in V1–V3, but V1 is not elevated: the RV branch is spared.

## Ecocardiografía

Inferior and inferoseptal walls abnormal, with a normal RV.
