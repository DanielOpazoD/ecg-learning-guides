# Subendocardial ischaemia

STE aVR: not OMI

Reperfusion, evolution & subendocardial

Diffuse STD, maximal in V4–V6, I and II, with STE in aVR is the signature of subendocardial ischaemia from demand, left main or three-vessel disease. The ST vector points into the cavity toward aVR, not at an occluded wall. It is high risk, but on its own it is neither OMI nor a STEMI equivalent.

Patrón: Diffuse subendocardial ischaemia (STE aVR)

## ECG

No STE in a coronary territory: either a normal ECG, or diffuse STD (maximal in V4–V6) with STE in aVR from subendocardial ischaemia.

## Ecocardiografía

No regional abnormality: normal, or mild global hypokinesis that follows no coronary distribution.
