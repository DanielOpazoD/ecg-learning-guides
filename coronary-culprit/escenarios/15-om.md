# Obtuse marginal

The "silent" OMI

Subtle & advanced OMI

An obtuse marginal supplies a patch of lateral wall that the standard leads barely see, so ST shifts stay under 1 mm. With ongoing ischaemic pain, an ECG like this does not exclude OMI. Use serial ECGs, posterior leads, echo for a wall-motion abnormality, and refractory symptoms.

Patrón: Electrically subtle ("silent") OMI

## ECG

STD maximal in V1–V3 (posterior wall seen from the front) with mild STE in I, aVL and V5–V6; the inferior leads change little.

## Ecocardiografía

Lateral and inferolateral walls abnormal; the inferior wall and septum are spared.
