# 29. Case 2 · plateau of 34

Session 3 · Case-based simulation

## Objetivo

Case 2 · ARDS

## Caso

An end-inspiratory pause measures a **plateau pressure of 34 cmH₂O**.

## Pregunta

What adjustments would you make?

## Respuesta del original

34 exceeds the recommended **< 30 cmH₂O** and increases the risk of ventilator-induced lung injury. **Reduce VT** (e.g. from 6 to 4–5 mL/kg PBW) to lower the plateau, then **reassess Pplat promptly**. A VT change will also change PaCO₂, so repeat the ABG.

## Actividades

1. Confirm the plateau with an **Insp hold**.

2. Lower VT (not below 4 mL/kg) and hold again until **Pplat < 30**.
