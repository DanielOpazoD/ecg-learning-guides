# 25. Safety features and alarms

Session 2 · Single-compartment model, loads and safety

## Objetivo

Describe and adjust safety features, including alarms

## Pregunta

What safety features are built into the ventilator, and how should alarms be set?

## Respuesta del original

Pressure limits, disconnect/low-pressure detection, apnea/backup ventilation, low minute-ventilation alarms, FiO₂ alarms and high-RR limits: they **prevent barotrauma** and **flag hypoventilation or disconnection**.

Set alarms relative to expected values: **high pressure** above the typical peak (catches obstruction/coughing), **low pressure/disconnect** near baseline, a **minute ventilation/VT floor** for hypoventilation, and an appropriate **apnea time**. Reassess after changes in mode, sedation or mechanics.

## Actividades

1. Set **Pmax** below the current peak pressure and watch the high-pressure alarm cut breaths short.

2. Disconnect the circuit (button below) and watch the alarm.
