# 19. When Pplat misleads

Session 2 · Single-compartment model, loads and safety

## Objetivo

Classify airway, alveolar and pleural pressure

## Pregunta

When is plateau pressure an unreliable surrogate for transpulmonary pressure?

## Respuesta del original

Pplat reflects the **whole respiratory system**: lung plus pleural cavity, chest wall and abdomen. It is unreliable whenever **pleural pressure is abnormal**: active expiration, obesity, or a large pleural effusion. Elevated pleural pressure raises the measured Pplat without raising the distending pressure across the lung.

Two patients with the same Pplat but different pleural pressures can have very different alveolar stress. **Esophageal manometry** estimates pleural pressure and gives a better transpulmonary pressure.

## Actividades

1. Do an **Insp hold**. Pplat looks worrying, but check **PL end-insp**.

2. Do an **Exp hold**: PL end-exp is negative (the lung is being squeezed shut). Raise PEEP until PL end-exp is about 0 or higher, then hold again.
