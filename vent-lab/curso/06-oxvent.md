# 06. Oxygenation vs ventilation

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Outline AC/PC, AC/VC and modifiable parameters

## Pregunta

Which parameters affect oxygenation and which affect ventilation?

## Respuesta del original

**PEEP and FiO₂ affect oxygenation.** PEEP works by raising **mean airway pressure**, the average airway pressure across the breath cycle.

**RR and VT (or inspiratory pressure) affect ventilation**, because CO₂ clearance follows minute ventilation (VT × RR).

The ABG panel in the monitor estimates the steady-state gas you would get with the current settings.

## Actividades

1. Increase the **RR** and watch the estimated **PaCO₂** fall.

2. Increase **PEEP** to 12 or more and watch **Pmean** and **PaO₂** rise.
