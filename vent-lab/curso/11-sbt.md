# 11. When PSV? What is an SBT?

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Review PSV and SBT

## Pregunta

In which scenarios is pressure support appropriate, and what is an SBT?

## Respuesta del original

PSV fits **SBTs, weaning, stable patients with a reliable respiratory drive**, or patients intubated solely to protect the upper airway.

An **SBT (spontaneous breathing trial)** is a time-limited test of extubation readiness: support is reduced to minimal levels (typically **PS 5, PEEP 5, FiO₂ 30%**) to see whether the patient can sustain ventilation and oxygenation without excessive work of breathing or hemodynamic instability.

## Actividades

1. Set up an SBT: **PS 5, PEEP 5, FiO₂ 30%**.
