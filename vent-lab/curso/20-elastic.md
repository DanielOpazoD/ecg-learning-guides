# 20. Stiff balloon: elastic load

Session 2 · Single-compartment model, loads and safety

## Objetivo

Distinguish the two components of ventilator load

## Pregunta

What if the balloon at the end of the straw is very stiff and difficult to inflate?

## Respuesta del original

An **increased elastic load** (decreased compliance; “elastic load” and “compliance” describe the same concept from opposite sides). Both **peak and plateau rise**, and the **difference between them stays normal** (assuming no added resistive load).

Causes: ARDS, pneumonia, pulmonary edema, atelectasis, fibrosis, auto-PEEP, right mainstem intubation, effusion, pneumothorax, obesity, ascites or abdominal compartment syndrome.
