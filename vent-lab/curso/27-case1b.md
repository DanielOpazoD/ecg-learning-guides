# 27. Case 1 · falling blood pressure

Session 3 · Case-based simulation

## Objetivo

Case 1 · COPD exacerbation

## Caso

A few minutes later the nurse notices the patient needs **escalating norepinephrine**. Look at the scalars.

## Pregunta

Describe what is happening based on the scalars, and how you would fix it.

## Respuesta del original

**Dynamic hyperinflation (auto-PEEP)** from insufficient expiratory time with obstructive physiology. Trapped gas raises end-expiratory alveolar and intrathoracic pressure, **impairs venous return** and causes hypotension. Make sure expiratory flow returns to baseline before each breath.

To lengthen expiratory time:

- **AC/VC and AC/PC:** lower the RR (consider sedation, even neuromuscular blockade, for patient-triggered tachypnea); treat the obstruction (bronchodilators).
- **AC/VC:** decrease VT (if > 6 mL/kg) and **increase inspiratory flow**; both shorten inspiration.
- **AC/PC:** decrease the set inspiratory time.
- An **I:E of 1:3 or more** is a reasonable start; adjust until expiratory flow reaches baseline.

This may worsen the acidosis, but with circulatory collapse looming, it is the least-harm trade.

## Actividades

1. Confirm it with an **Exp hold**.

2. Fix it, then prove it: **Exp hold** with auto-PEEP ≤ 2 and MAP ≥ 65.
