# 21. Blocked straw: resistive load

Session 2 · Single-compartment model, loads and safety

## Objetivo

Distinguish the two components of ventilator load

## Pregunta

What happens in the event of a partial blockage in the straw?

## Respuesta del original

An **increased resistive load**: **higher peak pressure**, but the plateau may stay similar if compliance is unchanged, so the **difference between peak and plateau increases** (≥ 10 cmH₂O).

Causes: obstructive airway disease/bronchospasm, kinked or bitten endotracheal tube, high flow rate or tidal volume, mucus plug or blood clot.
