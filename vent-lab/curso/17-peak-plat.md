# 17. Peak and plateau

Session 2 · Single-compartment model, loads and safety

## Objetivo

Classify airway, alveolar and pleural pressure

## Pregunta

What are peak and plateau pressures? How are they measured and what do they represent?

## Respuesta del original

Think of a **straw (airway) and a balloon (alveoli)**. Resistance to flow in a straw rises with its length and gas viscosity, and falls with the **fourth power of its radius** (Poiseuille).

**Peak pressure** combines the elastic load of the balloon and the resistive load of the straw. To measure only the balloon, remove resistance by stopping flow: an **end-inspiratory pause**. The pressure during that pause is the **plateau pressure**, reflecting alveolar/balloon pressure. In ARDS we aim for **Pplat < 30 cmH₂O**.

## Actividades

1. Press **Insp hold**. Read **Ppeak** and **Pplat**; the drop between them is the straw.

2. Raise **Peak flow** to 90 and hold again: the peak rises, the plateau doesn’t.
