# 05. Changeable parameters

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Outline AC/PC, AC/VC and modifiable parameters

## Pregunta

What are the changeable parameters in each mode?

## Respuesta del original

**Both AC/VC and AC/PC:** RR, PEEP and FiO₂.

**AC/VC:** VT, flow rate and flow pattern.

**AC/PC:** inspiratory pressure and inspiratory time.

Look at the tiles under the screen: they change with the mode.

## Actividades

1. In AC/VC, change the **VT**.

2. Switch to AC/PC and change **Pinsp** or **Ti**. Watch VT follow.
