# 16. PEEP

Session 2 · Single-compartment model, loads and safety

## Objetivo

Describe the single-compartment model of the lung

## Pregunta

Can you describe PEEP?

## Respuesta del original

PEEP is the **baseline positive pressure maintained at end-expiration** to prevent alveolar collapse and improve oxygenation. It may increase mean airway pressure and **can reduce venous return** if it is too high.

## Actividades

1. Raise **PEEP** to 14. Watch the balloon stay bigger at end-expiration and the estimated PaO₂ climb.

2. Push PEEP to 22 and watch **MAP** fall: less venous return.
