# 23. Auto-PEEP

Session 2 · Single-compartment model, loads and safety

## Objetivo

Understand auto-PEEP

## Pregunta

What is auto-PEEP, how do you identify it, and how do you quantify it?

## Respuesta del original

Auto-PEEP comes from **incomplete exhalation**: progressive air trapping and higher intrathoracic pressure, above the set PEEP. It can **impair venous return, reduce cardiac output** and raise the risk of barotrauma. As it worsens, peak and plateau both rise.

**Identify:** expiratory flow **does not return to baseline** before the next breath.

**Quantify:** an **expiratory hold** measures total PEEP; auto-PEEP = total PEEP − set PEEP.

## Actividades

1. Press **Exp hold** and read total PEEP and auto-PEEP.

2. Fix it: change settings until an **Exp hold** shows auto-PEEP ≤ 2.
