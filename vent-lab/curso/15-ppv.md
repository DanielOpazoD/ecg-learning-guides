# 15. Positive-pressure breath

Session 2 · Single-compartment model, loads and safety

## Objetivo

Describe the single-compartment model of the lung

## Pregunta

How does a ventilator-supported positive-pressure breath drive air into the alveoli?

## Respuesta del original

The ventilator **raises airway pressure above alveolar pressure**, so air flows into the lungs. Under normal conditions **pleural pressure rises** during inspiration: the opposite of a spontaneous breath.

1. Watch **Pes** rise with each ventilator breath, then take a breath yourself between them and watch it fall instead.
