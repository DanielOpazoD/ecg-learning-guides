# 18. Airway, alveolar, pleural

Session 2 · Single-compartment model, loads and safety

## Objetivo

Classify airway, alveolar and pleural pressure

## Pregunta

How do airway, alveolar and pleural pressures differ in the single-compartment model?

## Respuesta del original

**Airway pressure** is measured at the airway opening. **Alveolar pressure** is the pressure within the alveoli (approximated by Pplat). **Pleural pressure** surrounds the lung and can be estimated with **esophageal manometry**.

**Transpulmonary pressure (PL)** = alveolar − pleural: the distending pressure across the lung itself.

## Actividades

1. Do an **Insp hold** and read **PL end-insp** in the balloon panel (Pplat − Pes).

2. Do an **Exp hold** and read **PL end-exp**.
