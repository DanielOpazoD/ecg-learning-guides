# 22. Fibrosis vs asthma on AC/VC

Session 2 · Single-compartment model, loads and safety

## Objetivo

Interpret peak and plateau pressures

## Pregunta

What would the scalars look like in pulmonary fibrosis versus asthma on AC/VC?

## Respuesta del original

**Fibrosis (decreased lung compliance):** high plateau with a correspondingly high peak; normal peak-plateau difference. **Expiratory flow is fast** and returns to baseline quickly.

**Asthma (increased airway resistance):** high peak with a relatively lower plateau. **Expiratory flow is slow** and needs more time to return to baseline.

## Actividades

1. Do an **Insp hold** on the fibrosis patient.

2. Load asthma, do an **Insp hold**, and compare the expiratory flow.
