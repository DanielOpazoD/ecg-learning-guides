# 13. Apnea on PSV

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Review PSV and SBT

## Pregunta

What would happen if a patient on PSV became apneic?

## Respuesta del original

If there is no backup mode, **ventilation stops**. Most ventilators activate **apnea/backup ventilation** and switch to an assist-control mode after a set apnea time.

Here the apnea time is on the settings tiles. Backup ventilation hands control back once the patient triggers again.

## Actividades

1. Sedate the patient: press the button (or switch off spontaneous breathing) and wait for the **APNEA** alarm and backup ventilation.

## Acciones interactivas

- Give a big dose of sedation
- Wake up
