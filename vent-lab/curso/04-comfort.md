# 04. Which mode is more comfortable?

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Outline AC/PC, AC/VC and modifiable parameters

## Pregunta

Which mode is more comfortable for the patient?

## Respuesta del original

Comfort depends on many factors. In general, **AC/PC often “feels” smoother** because its exponentially decaying flow closely mirrors the flow of our own negative-pressure breaths.

A **well-set AC/VC can be equally comfortable**. Here the patient is pulling hard and the set flow is low: watch the pressure scalar scoop down during inspiration.

## Actividades

1. Raise **Peak flow** to 60 L/min or more and watch the scooped pressure fill back in.

2. Now switch to **AC/PC** and compare how flow follows the patient’s pull.
