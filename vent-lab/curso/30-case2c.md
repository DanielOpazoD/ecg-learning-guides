# 30. Case 2 · permissive hypercapnia

Session 3 · Case-based simulation

## Objetivo

Case 2 · ARDS

## Caso

After VT is finalized, the repeat ABG at 30 minutes shows **pH 7.21, PaCO₂ 63, PaO₂ 84** on FiO₂ 80%.

## Pregunta

Should you make any changes?

## Respuesta del original

In ARDS we prioritize low lung volumes and tolerate **permissive hypercapnia** to keep Pplat < 30. With severe hypercapnic acidemia (**pH < 7.20**), **raise the RR first**, reserving VT increases for refractory cases while staying lung-protective.

An excessively high RR can itself be injurious and can cause auto-PEEP. **A normal ABG is not always the goal in ARDS.**

## Actividades

1. Raise the **RR** (not VT) and watch the estimated pH.

2. Check that flow still returns to baseline: **Exp hold** with auto-PEEP ≤ 2.
