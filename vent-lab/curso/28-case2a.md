# 28. Case 2 · mode and initial settings

Session 3 · Case-based simulation

## Objetivo

Case 2 · ARDS

## Caso

**A 50-year-old woman** with multifocal pneumonia, severe hypoxemia and hypercapnia is intubated; imaging and ABG are consistent with **ARDS**. Height **5′10″ (70 in)**.

## Pregunta

Which mode would you use, and what initial settings?

## Respuesta del original

No mode is inherently superior, but the landmark lung-protective ARDS trials used **AC/VC**, which guarantees low VT and reliable plateau monitoring. AC/PC can work but holding a stable 6 mL/kg needs close monitoring. The true therapy is **lung-protective ventilation**.

- **PBW** for a 70-inch female ≈ **68.5 kg**.
- **VT 6 mL/kg ≈ 410 mL**.
- **RR ~18–24**, titrated to a safe I:E (typically 1:2).
- **PEEP/FiO₂ from the ARDSNet table**, e.g. FiO₂ 80% with PEEP 14. Wean FiO₂ to ≤ 60% as soon as feasible.

## Actividades

1. Choose **AC/VC**.

2. Set **VT ≈ 6 mL/kg PBW** (400–420 mL), **RR 18–24**, **PEEP 14**, **FiO₂ 80%**.
