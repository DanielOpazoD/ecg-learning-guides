# 26. Case 1 · hypercapnia

Session 3 · Case-based simulation

## Objetivo

Case 1 · COPD exacerbation

## Caso

**A 65-year-old man with a COPD exacerbation** is intubated for worsening hypercapnia and altered mental status. AC/VC: **VT 450 mL (~6 mL/kg PBW), RR 14, FiO₂ 60%, PEEP 8**.
ABG at 30 minutes: **pH 7.25, PaCO₂ 65, PaO₂ 65** on FiO₂ 60%.

## Pregunta

What is the primary problem, and which setting(s) should you adjust?

## Respuesta del original

**Hypercapnia** (low pH, high PaCO₂). Ventilation follows minute ventilation (VT × RR), so increase one of them. VT is already lung-protective, so a safe change is to **increase the RR**.

Alternatively, with no acute lung injury requiring strict limits, **VT can go to 8 mL/kg PBW provided Pplat stays < 30**. Beware: a higher RR can cause **dynamic hyperinflation** in obstructive disease.

## Actividades

1. Increase minute ventilation (RR or VT) and watch the estimated PaCO₂ improve.
