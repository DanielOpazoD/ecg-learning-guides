# 01. Why ventilate?

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Understand the purpose and indications of MV

## Pregunta

Why do some patients require mechanical ventilation, and what are the indications?

## Respuesta del original

MV is used when a patient **cannot maintain adequate oxygenation, ventilation, or work of breathing** despite noninvasive support, or when **airway protection** is needed. It supports gas exchange and reduces respiratory muscle load while the underlying process is treated.

Common indications: **refractory hypoxemia, hypercapnic respiratory failure, excessive work of breathing/fatigue, or airway protection.**

The ventilator is not only a supportive therapy, it is also **a tool that gives you information**. Everything in this lab is read from three scalars over time: pressure, flow and volume.

## Actividades

1. Find the three scalars on the screen: **pressure** (yellow), **flow** (teal) and **volume** (green). Freeze the screen with **Freeze** (or F) to look closely.

2. Hover over (or tap, when frozen) a point on the scalars to read its exact values.
