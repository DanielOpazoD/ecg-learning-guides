# 08. Trigger, inspiration, cycle, expiration

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Outline AC/PC, AC/VC and modifiable parameters

## Pregunta

Which portions of the breath are trigger, inspiration, cycle and expiration?

## Respuesta del original

**Trigger:** initiates inspiration (the patient, or time based on the RR).

**Inspiration:** flow into the lungs.

**Cycle:** initiates expiration.

**Expiration:** flow out of the lungs. Like a pulled spring that is let go, the lungs recoil inward **passively**.

The phase bands shade inspiration teal. Grey triangles mark time-triggered breaths.

## Actividades

1. Freeze the screen and hover over a breath. Find the moment the flow crosses zero going down: that is the **cycle**.

2. Make inspiration longer (raise **Ti**) and watch expiration get shorter.
