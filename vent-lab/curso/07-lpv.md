# 07. Lung-protective ventilation

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Outline AC/PC, AC/VC and modifiable parameters

## Pregunta

What is lung-protective ventilation, and what are the goal modifiable parameters?

## Respuesta del original

A collective set of strategies to reduce injury to the lung. The key takeaway at this point: **VT should be ≤ 6 mL/kg predicted body weight (PBW)**, not actual weight. There are also goal pressures to maintain, covered in Session 2.

The VT tile shows mL/kg PBW under the value. PBW comes from sex and height (side panel).

## Actividades

1. Set **VT** to 6 mL/kg PBW or less.

2. Press the button to make the patient a 5′2″ woman and see how the same VT becomes too big.
