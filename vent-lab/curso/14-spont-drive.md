# 14. How does spontaneous effort move air?

Session 2 · Single-compartment model, loads and safety

## Objetivo

Describe the single-compartment model of the lung

## Pregunta

How does spontaneous inspiratory effort drive air into the alveoli?

## Respuesta del original

The patient generates **negative pleural pressure**, which lowers alveolar pressure “below” airway pressure, and air flows into the lungs. Because the alveolar space is connected to atmosphere, flow occurs so rapidly that **alveolar pressure stays near atmospheric**.

Watch the digital lung: Ppl (pleural, measured by the balloon as Pes) swings well negative while Palv barely dips.

1. Take a breath with **Breathe IN**. Compare how far **Ppl** and **Palv** fall on the digital lung.
