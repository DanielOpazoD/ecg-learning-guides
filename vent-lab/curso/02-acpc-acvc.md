# 02. AC/PC vs AC/VC

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Outline AC/PC, AC/VC and modifiable parameters

## Pregunta

What is the difference between AC/PC and AC/VC?

## Respuesta del original

Both are **assist-control** modes in which you set the RR, FiO₂ and PEEP. In assist-control the ventilator delivers a **full preset breath** whether it is triggered by the patient or by the backup rate.

In **AC/VC**, tidal volume is set and **pressure varies**. In **AC/PC**, inspiratory pressure is set and **tidal volume varies**.

You can often name the mode from the scalars alone: AC/VC has a **square (or decelerating ramp) flow** with a rising pressure; AC/PC has a **square pressure** with an exponentially decaying flow. In this curriculum we stick to square and decelerating-ramp flow in AC/VC.

## Actividades

1. Open **MODE** and start **AC/PC**. Notice which scalar becomes square.

2. Go back to **AC/VC** and switch the **Flow pattern** tile to **Decel ramp**.
