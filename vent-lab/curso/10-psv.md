# 10. Pressure support

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Review PSV and SBT

## Pregunta

What is pressure support mode, and how does it differ from assist-control?

## Respuesta del original

PSV is a **patient-triggered, pressure-supported** mode in which you set the inspiratory pressure, PEEP and FiO₂. The **RR is solely the patient’s**.

Each breath ends when inspiratory flow falls to a set fraction of its peak (the cycle threshold), so the patient also controls inspiratory time.

## Actividades

1. Lower **PS** and watch VT fall while the rate stays the patient’s.

2. Raise **PS** to 15 or more and watch VT climb.
