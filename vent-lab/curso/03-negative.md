# 03. What “mode” do we breathe in?

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Outline AC/PC, AC/VC and modifiable parameters

## Pregunta

What “mode” do we breathe in when we breathe spontaneously?

## Respuesta del original

We breathe with **negative pressure** generated by our respiratory muscles. The ventilator provides breaths with **positive pressure**.

If we had to match a ventilator mode to spontaneous breathing, **pressure-targeted modes are more similar** than volume-targeted modes.

## Actividades

1. Hold **Breathe IN** (or the space bar). Watch pleural pressure (Pes) go negative while airway pressure stays at zero.

2. Keep holding for more than 3 seconds. Your muscles can’t contract forever: the breath ends on its own.
