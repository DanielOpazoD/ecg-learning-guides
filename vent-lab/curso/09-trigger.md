# 09. Patient vs time trigger

Session 1 · Indications, basic modes and scalars

## Objetivo

Detect patient efforts

## Pregunta

How can you distinguish patient-triggered from time-triggered breaths?

## Respuesta del original

The ventilator **marks patient efforts that result in a breath**. The mark varies from ventilator to ventilator. Here, patient-triggered breaths get an **orange** triangle and time-triggered breaths a **grey** one.

Look also for the small dip in pressure (or rise in flow) just before the breath: that is the patient’s effort reaching the trigger threshold.

## Actividades

1. Hold **Breathe IN** during expiration to trigger a breath yourself. Look for the orange mark.

2. Switch the trigger tile to **Pressure** and set it to 5 cmH₂O, then trigger again. It takes a much bigger pull.
