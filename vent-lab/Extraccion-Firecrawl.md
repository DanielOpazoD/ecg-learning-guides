## How to use Vent Lab

A ventilator attached to a digital single-compartment lung. Everything you see is computed from the equation of motion, so what you change is what you get.

✕

**Change settings.** Tap a tile, then turn the knob, scroll, use the arrow keys, or press + and −. Changes take effect on the next breath, like a real ventilator.

**Be the patient.** Hold **Breathe IN** (or the space bar) to contract your inspiratory muscles. You can’t hold forever: the ring is your stamina. Hold **Breathe OUT** (E) for active expiration.

**Measure.** _Insp hold_ stops flow at end-inspiration to show plateau pressure. _Exp hold_ closes both valves at end-expiration to show total PEEP (and auto-PEEP).

**Read the screen.** _Freeze_ (F) stops the sweep; hover or tap the scalars to read any point. Turn on the esophageal balloon, Pmus, load shading and phase bands in the side panel.

**Change the patient.** Pick ARDS, COPD, asthma, fibrosis, obesity or a plugged tube, or drag resistance and compliance yourself. Add spontaneous breathing, reverse triggering, a leak or cardiac oscillations.

**Learn, then play.** _Learn the basics_ walks through the curriculum step by step. The _Asynchrony Challenge_ is a 5-minute scored game with a leaderboard.

For education only. A teaching model with simplified physiology (single compartment, no recruitment dynamics, estimated gas exchange); it is not a clinical decision tool.
