# 07. tau

Dificultad: easy

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

Passive patient on AC/PC. Look at how quickly flow decays.

## Pregunta

What does the flow scalar tell you?

1. Flow is square: volume control
2. Flow reaches zero fast: short time constant, low compliance
3. Flow decays slowly: long time constant, high resistance
4. Flow shows patient effort

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Flow decays slowly: long time constant, high resistance

## Explicación

Flow takes a long time to decay in both directions and may not reach zero: a long time constant (τ = R × C), which in practice usually means high resistance.

## Qué observar

One τ empties 63%; about 5τ empties the lung. Normal intubated τ ≈ 0.5 s.
