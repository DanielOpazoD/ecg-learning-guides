# 01. mode

Dificultad: easy

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

You walk into the room and the settings screen is hidden. Read the scalars.

## Pregunta

Which mode most likely produced these waveforms?

1. AC/PC (PC-CMVs)
2. PSV (PC-CSVs)
3. AC/VC (VC-CMVs)
4. APRV (PC-IMV(1)s,s)

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

PSV (PC-CSVs)

## Explicación

Pressure is square (pressure control), but every breath is patient-triggered (orange marks), the rate and Ti vary breath to breath and each breath ends when flow decays: continuous spontaneous ventilation.

## Qué observar

Step 1 of reading waveforms: define the mode. Which variable is controlled (square), and are breaths triggered by time or by the patient?
