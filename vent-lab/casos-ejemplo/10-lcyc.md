# 10. lcyc

Dificultad: medium

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

COPD on PSV with the cycle threshold at 5%. The patient is contracting the abdomen at the end of each breath.

## Pregunta

Which patient-ventilator interaction is this?

1. Multiple (double) trigger
2. Late trigger
3. Early cycle
4. Late cycle

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Late cycle

## Explicación

The breath keeps going after the patient has stopped inhaling and starts to push: there is a pressure spike at end-inspiration and flow drops abruptly before the ventilator finally cycles. The patient’s neural inspiration is shorter than the ventilator’s.

## Qué observar

End-inspiratory pressure overshoot and a steep fall in flow before cycling.
