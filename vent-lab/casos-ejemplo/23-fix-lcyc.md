Dificultad: hard

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

COPD on PSV 16 with ETS 5%. He is fighting the end of every breath.

## Pregunta

What is the best adjustment?

1. Increase pressure support
2. Lengthen the rise time
3. Raise the cycle threshold (ETS) so breaths end sooner
4. Lower the cycle threshold further

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Raise the cycle threshold (ETS) so breaths end sooner

## Explicación

Late cycle: with a long time constant, flow decays slowly and a 5% threshold keeps the breath going long after the patient stops inhaling, so he pushes against it. Raising ETS (often 40–50% in COPD) ends the breath closer to neural Ti. Lowering PS can also help; more PS makes it worse.

## Qué observar

End-inspiratory pressure spike, abrupt flow drop.
