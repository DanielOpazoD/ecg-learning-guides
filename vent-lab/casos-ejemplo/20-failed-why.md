# 20. failed-why

Dificultad: hard

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

Some of this patient’s efforts are not being answered by the ventilator.

## Pregunta

What is the most likely cause of the failed triggers?

1. Cardiac oscillations
2. Trigger threshold set too high: flow crosses zero but not the threshold
3. Circuit leak
4. Auto-PEEP: the effort can’t even bring flow to zero

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Auto-PEEP: the effort can’t even bring flow to zero

## Explicación

The missed efforts deflect expiratory flow toward baseline but never reach zero, and expiration hasn’t finished before each breath. The patient must first overcome auto-PEEP before any inspiratory flow can occur.

## Qué observar

Auto-PEEP: bump toward zero. High threshold: flow crosses zero but stays below the trigger level.
