# 13. early

Dificultad: medium

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

Deeply sedated ARDS patient on AC/VC; every breath is time-triggered.

## Pregunta

Which patient-ventilator interaction is this?

1. Early trigger
2. False trigger
3. Late cycle
4. Late trigger

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Early trigger

## Explicación

A machine-triggered breath comes first, then the patient’s effort follows during inspiration or early expiration (see the Pes dip and the pressure/flow deformation). The inflation is entraining the effort: early trigger, also called reverse triggering.

## Qué observar

Time-triggered breath, then evidence of Pmus. If the effort lasts into expiration it can trigger a second, stacked breath.
