# 27. fix-early

Dificultad: hard

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

Deeply sedated ARDS patient (RASS −5). Breath stacking is pushing Pplat over 30.

## Pregunta

What is the most appropriate response?

1. Make the trigger more sensitive
2. Increase VT so the patient is satisfied
3. Switch to PSV
4. Recognize reverse triggering; adjust sedation or the set rate/Ti, and consider deeper sedation or paralysis if stacking persists

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Recognize reverse triggering; adjust sedation or the set rate/Ti, and consider deeper sedation or paralysis if stacking persists

## Explicación

These are early triggers: time-triggered breaths entrain an effort that carries into expiration and stacks a second breath, delivering injurious volumes. Options include changing the rate or Ti to break the entrainment and adjusting sedation; when stacking threatens lung protection, deeper sedation or neuromuscular blockade may be needed. Trigger sensitivity is irrelevant: the first breath isn’t patient-triggered.

## Qué observar

Machine breath first, effort second.
