# 04. failed

Dificultad: easy

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

COPD patient on AC/VC who looks like he is breathing faster than the ventilator rate.

## Pregunta

Which patient-ventilator interaction is this?

1. Failed trigger
2. Early trigger
3. False trigger
4. Late cycle

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Failed trigger

## Explicación

During expiration the flow scalar shows bumps toward baseline that never trigger a breath: the patient’s effort fails to reach the trigger. Here the cause is auto-PEEP: the effort must first overcome trapped pressure before flow can even cross zero.

## Qué observar

Expiratory flow deflects toward zero (and airway pressure dips) without a breath following.
