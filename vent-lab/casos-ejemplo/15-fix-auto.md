# 15. fix-auto

Dificultad: medium

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

COPD on AC/VC, now on rising norepinephrine.

## Pregunta

What is the best ventilator change?

1. Add an inspiratory pause to measure plateau
2. Lower the RR and raise inspiratory flow to lengthen expiration
3. Raise the RR to clear more CO₂
4. Increase VT to 8 mL/kg

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Lower the RR and raise inspiratory flow to lengthen expiration

## Explicación

Expiratory flow is not returning to zero: auto-PEEP is compromising venous return. Give the lung more time to empty: lower the rate (sedation if the patient is driving it), and in AC/VC raise inspiratory flow and/or lower VT to shorten inspiration. Bronchodilate. Accept more hypercapnia if needed.

## Qué observar

An I:E of 1:3 or more is a reasonable start; adjust until expiratory flow returns to baseline.
