# 14. expw

Dificultad: medium

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

Anxious, with visible abdominal muscle contraction during exhalation.

## Pregunta

Which patient-ventilator interaction is this?

1. Late trigger
2. Expiratory work
3. Early cycle
4. Failed trigger

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Expiratory work

## Explicación

Mid-expiration the flow moves away from baseline (a second, deeper expiratory hump) and Pes rises: the patient is actively pushing gas out. That is expiratory work; it also makes Pplat unreliable, because pleural pressure is raised.

## Qué observar

Expiratory flow deflecting away from baseline instead of decaying smoothly.
