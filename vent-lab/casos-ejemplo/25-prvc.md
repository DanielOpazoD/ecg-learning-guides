Dificultad: hard

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

PRVC (PC-CMVa), target VT 450. The patient is getting more agitated; the delivered pressure keeps falling breath after breath.

## Pregunta

Why is the inspiratory pressure falling?

1. Adaptive targeting: as the patient pulls harder, the ventilator gives less pressure to hold VT at target
2. A circuit leak
3. Compliance is improving
4. Auto-PEEP

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Adaptive targeting: as the patient pulls harder, the ventilator gives less pressure to hold VT at target

## Explicación

PRVC adjusts pressure between breaths to hit the target VT. When the patient’s effort adds volume, the ventilator lowers its pressure, so the work shifts to the patient. In severe cases support falls to near PEEP while VT stays above target, all generated by the patient.

## Qué observar

Adaptive modes: total work is not constant, and support falls as effort rises.
