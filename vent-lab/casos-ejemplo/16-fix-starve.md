# 16. fix-starve

Dificultad: medium

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

Metabolic acidosis, strong drive, AC/VC at 30 L/min.

## Pregunta

What is the best next step?

1. Decrease the peak flow
2. Add an end-inspiratory pause
3. Increase inspiratory flow (or use a pressure-targeted mode) and treat the high drive
4. Make the trigger less sensitive

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Increase inspiratory flow (or use a pressure-targeted mode) and treat the high drive

## Explicación

Pressure dips below PEEP during inspiration: severe work shifting. The set flow is far below the patient’s demand. Give more flow (or a pressure-targeted mode, which lets flow follow effort) and address why the drive is so high: pain, anxiety, acidosis, hypoxemia. When drive is extreme no setting fixes it; sedation may be needed.

## Qué observar

Scooped inspiratory pressure in volume control.
