# 22. multi-why

Dificultad: hard

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

Double triggering on AC/VC. The esophageal balloon is in place.

## Pregunta

What is causing the multiple triggers?

1. Early trigger (reverse triggering): the machine breath comes first, then the effort
2. False trigger
3. Late cycle
4. Early cycle: one long effort spans both breaths

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Early trigger (reverse triggering): the machine breath comes first, then the effort

## Explicación

The first breath is time-triggered (grey) with a flat Pes; the patient’s effort (Pes dip) begins after inflation starts and carries into expiration, triggering the stacked breath. That is early trigger.

## Qué observar

Multiple trigger is a pattern with at least three causes: early cycle, early trigger and false trigger. Timing of Pmus tells them apart.
