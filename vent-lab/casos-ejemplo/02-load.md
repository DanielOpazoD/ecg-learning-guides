# 02. load

Dificultad: easy

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

AC/VC with a 0.5-second end-inspiratory pause on every breath.

## Pregunta

What is the dominant load?

1. Normal loads
2. Increased resistive load
3. Patient inspiratory effort
4. Increased elastic load (low compliance)

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Increased resistive load

## Explicación

There is a big drop from peak to plateau (≥ 10 cmH₂O) with a near-normal plateau: the straw is narrow. Think bronchospasm, a kinked or bitten tube, secretions.

## Qué observar

During the pause flow is zero, so resistance drops out and only the elastic (alveolar) pressure remains.
