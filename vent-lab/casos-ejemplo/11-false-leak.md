# 11. false-leak

Dificultad: medium

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

Just received a propofol bolus and is not moving. Yet the ventilator rate is high.

## Pregunta

Which patient-ventilator interaction is this?

1. False trigger
2. Early trigger
3. Normal synchrony
4. Failed trigger

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

False trigger

## Explicación

Breaths are flagged as patient-triggered, but there is no sign of patient effort. Volume never returns to zero before the next breath and resets abruptly (the “square-root sign”): gas is leaking, the bias flow that replaces it looks like an inspiratory effort, and it trips the flow trigger.

## Qué observar

Patient-triggered breaths with no evidence of Pmus, plus an inspired-expired volume mismatch.
