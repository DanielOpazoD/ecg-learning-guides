# 18. fix-leak

Dificultad: medium

Ejemplo generado por el motor original. La plantilla puede producir otras variantes.

## Situación

Sedated patient on PSV with an unexpectedly high rate. You hear a gurgle at the mouth.

## Pregunta

What is the best next step?

1. Find and fix the leak (cuff, circuit); make the flow trigger less sensitive meanwhile
2. Make the trigger more sensitive
3. Increase pressure support
4. Deepen sedation

## Respuesta del original para este ejemplo

Find and fix the leak (cuff, circuit); make the flow trigger less sensitive meanwhile

## Explicación

False triggers from a leak (volume never returns to zero; no patient effort). Fix the leak: check cuff pressure and the circuit. A less sensitive trigger (or leak compensation) stops the auto-triggering. Sedation won’t help: the patient isn’t making these breaths.

## Qué observar

Square-root sign on the volume scalar.
