Each guideline value on this page is cited to its source. The cut-off values are taken from the American Society of Echocardiography (ASE), the European Association of Cardiovascular Imaging (EACVI), the British Society of Echocardiography (BSE), the ACC/AHA, the ESC and the ESC/ERS.

The figures are generated by simple models calibrated to agree with these sources. The diastolic values and the Doppler and strain values of the amyloid heart are illustrative and are not patient data. All images are simulated, including the drawings, the heart model and the echocardiographic views.
