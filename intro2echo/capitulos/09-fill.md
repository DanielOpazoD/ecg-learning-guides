## Diastolic function

Filling and LA pressure

LV diastolic function determines the LA pressure that is required to fill the LV. As LV relaxation slows and the LV then stiffens, the mitral inflow, the annular velocities, the pulmonary venous flow and the LA change together. The 2025 ASE algorithm classifies the LA pressure and grades diastolic dysfunction.

### Grades of diastolic dysfunction

1. In **grade 1** diastolic dysfunction, LV relaxation is slowed; e′ decreases, and E/A decreases to 0.8 or less. E/e′ and the PA pressure remain normal, and the LA pressure is therefore normal[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).
2. In **grade 2**, the LA pressure is mildly to moderately raised and E/A remains below 2, so the mitral inflow can appear normal again, a pattern termed pseudonormal[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17). E/e′ and the peak TR velocity increase, the pulmonary vein S/D ratio decreases to 0.67 or less, and LA reservoir strain decreases to 18% or less[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).
3. **Grade 3** is defined by a markedly raised LA pressure with E/A ≥2, the restrictive filling pattern[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17). In patients with a normal LVEF, a restrictive pattern with a dilated LA is associated with a poor prognosis[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).

The values in the figure are illustrative. In patients with a normal LVEF, the pulmonary vein S/D ratio can remain above 0.67 although the filling pressures are high; an LA reservoir strain ≤18% is specific for a raised LA pressure, but a higher strain does not exclude it[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).

### Doppler variables

- The mitral inflow **E and A** velocities represent early diastolic filling and atrial filling ( [chapter 05](https://intro2echo.com/cycle)). Slow LV relaxation reduces early filling relative to atrial filling, so E/A decreases[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).
- The early diastolic velocity of the mitral annulus, **e′**, is measured with tissue Doppler. It depends directly on LV relaxation and is therefore the first variable in the algorithm[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).
- The ratio **E/e′** increases with LA pressure. In an early study, lateral E/e′ correlated with the pulmonary capillary wedge pressure (r = 0.87)[42](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-42).
- In the absence of lung or pulmonary vascular disease, a **peak TR velocity** of 2.8 m/s or more supports a raised LA pressure[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).

### The 2025 ASE algorithm in sinus rhythm

1. **Step 1** assesses three variables: reduced e′ (septal ≤6, lateral ≤7 or average ≤6.5 cm/s); raised E/e′ (septal ≥15, lateral ≥13 or average ≥14); and a peak TR velocity ≥2.8 m/s, or a PA systolic pressure ≥35 mmHg ( [chapter 07](https://intro2echo.com/pressure)) when the RA pressure is known[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17). [Chapter 07](https://intro2echo.com/pressure) estimates the RA pressure from the IVC.
2. **Step 2** depends on which step 1 variables are abnormal. If all three are normal, the LA pressure and diastolic function are normal. If reduced e′ alone is abnormal and E/A is ≤0.8, the LA pressure is normal, with grade 1 diastolic dysfunction. In a symptomatic patient with a normal LA pressure at rest, diastolic exercise echocardiography is recommended. If all three are abnormal, the LA pressure is raised. Reduced e′ alone with E/A >0.8, raised E/e′ alone, a raised peak TR velocity alone and any two abnormal variables require step 3[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).
3. **Step 3** assesses the pulmonary vein S/D ratio (≤0.67), LA reservoir strain (≤18%) and the LA volume index (>34 mL/m²). Figure 3 lists IVRT ≤70 ms as an alternative variable; in a patient with heart disease, an IVRT this short suggests a raised LA pressure. One or more abnormal variables indicate a raised LA pressure. If none is abnormal, the LA pressure is normal, and the algorithm assigns no grade[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).
4. In **step 4**, E/A grades a raised LA pressure: E/A <2 indicates grade 2 and E/A ≥2 indicates grade 3 diastolic dysfunction[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).

In the diagram above, a change in one measurement moves the slider to a band of the new result. A move of the slider returns every value to the model.

In a validation study of 951 patients with catheter-measured pressures, 2 patients remained unclassified by the algorithm (38 by the 2016 approach), and the accuracy of the algorithm was 86%[43](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-43).

### Exclusions and limitations

The algorithm should not be applied in atrial fibrillation, mitral stenosis, severe primary mitral regurgitation or moderate or severe mitral annular calcification, after mitral valve repair or replacement or heart transplantation, or in patients with an LV assist device, pericardial constriction or pulmonary hypertension of non-cardiac cause[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17). The guideline does not apply to children, to normal pregnancy or to patients during surgery[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).

If the grade is uncertain, the report should state whether the LA pressure is normal or raised[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17). The e′ velocity decreases with age, and age-adjusted limits can be used in place of the fixed cut-off values[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).

In advanced cardiac amyloidosis, the mitral annular s′, e′ and a′ velocities can all be below 5 cm/s, a finding termed the “5-5-5” sign[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17) ( [bonus chapter B4](https://intro2echo.com/amyloid)).

### Key points

1. Step 1 of the algorithm assesses e′, E/e′ and the peak TR velocity.
2. If step 1 is not conclusive, step 3 assesses the pulmonary vein S/D ratio, LA reservoir strain and the LA volume index.
3. When the LA pressure is raised, E/A determines the grade of diastolic dysfunction.

## Cuestionario del original

### 1. A patient has a reduced e′, a normal E/e′, a normal peak TR velocity and an E/A of 0.7. What is the classification by the algorithm?

1. Grade 1 with normal LA pressure
2. Grade 2
3. Normal diastolic function

**Respuesta del original:** Grade 1 with normal LA pressure

**Explicación:** Reduced e′ is the only abnormal first-step variable and E/A is ≤0.8, so the classification is grade 1 diastolic dysfunction with normal LA pressure.

### 2. All three first-step variables are abnormal, and E/A is 2.5. What is the grade of diastolic dysfunction?

1. Grade 2
2. Grade 3
3. Cannot be graded

**Respuesta del original:** Grade 3

**Explicación:** Three abnormal first-step variables indicate a raised LA pressure, and a raised LA pressure with E/A ≥2 is classified as grade 3 diastolic dysfunction.

### 3. In which of the following patients should this algorithm not be applied?

1. Atrial fibrillation
2. Hypertension
3. Age 70 years

**Respuesta del original:** Atrial fibrillation

**Explicación:** The main algorithm applies to sinus rhythm; the guideline provides a separate approach for atrial fibrillation.
