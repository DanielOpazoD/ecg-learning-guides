## Cycle

The cardiac cycle

Mitral and aortic valve opening and closure divide the cardiac cycle into isovolumic contraction, ejection, isovolumic relaxation and filling. Echocardiography times each event from the ECG, from valve motion and from Doppler flow.

### The Wiggers diagram

The Wiggers diagram plots LV pressure, LV volume, LA pressure and aortic pressure against time and marks mitral and aortic valve opening and closure[12](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-12). Mitral valve closure coincides with the first heart sound (S1), and aortic valve closure coincides with the second heart sound (S2)[12](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-12). Electrical events precede mechanical events; for example, the QRS complex precedes ventricular contraction[12](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-12).

### Phase durations

In 1,969 healthy adults, the isovolumic contraction time is 40 ± 10 ms, the ejection time 292 ± 23 ms and the isovolumic relaxation time 96 ± 19 ms[13](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-13). The model in the figure uses these mean values at a heart rate of 70 beats per minute. The isovolumic relaxation time increases with age[13](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-13).

### Echocardiographic timing

- **End-diastole** is the first frame after mitral valve closure or the frame with the largest LV dimension; the QRS complex or the R wave can be used instead[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14), [15](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-15).
- **End-systole** is the frame at or after aortic valve closure[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14) and lies near the end of the T wave[16](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-16).
- In the **mitral inflow** velocity recording, the E wave peaks after the T wave, and the A wave occurs after the P wave[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).
- **IVRT** extends from the end of LVOT flow to the onset of mitral inflow. It is recorded with CW Doppler through the LVOT in the apical five-chamber or apical long-axis view, so that the LVOT outflow and mitral inflow signals are displayed together[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17).

IVRT lengthens with age, so a single normal value should not be applied across all ages[17](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-17). [Chapter 09](https://intro2echo.com/fill) uses a short IVRT as a sign of a raised LA pressure.

Most of the LV filling volume enters during early diastolic filling; atrial contraction contributes the remainder, about one fifth of the filling volume in the example of Mitchell and Wang[12](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-12).

### Key points

1. Mitral valve closure coincides with S1, and aortic valve closure coincides with S2.
2. End-diastole is the first frame after mitral valve closure, and end-systole is the frame at or after aortic valve closure.
3. IVRT is measured from the end of LVOT flow to the onset of the mitral E wave.

## Cuestionario del original

### 1. Which valve event of the left heart coincides with the first heart sound?

1. Mitral valve closure
2. Aortic valve opening
3. Aortic valve closure

**Respuesta del original:** Mitral valve closure

**Explicación:** Mitral valve closure coincides with the first heart sound (S1), and aortic valve closure coincides with the second heart sound (S2).

### 2. On Doppler recordings, the IVRT is measured from…

1. the end of LVOT flow to the onset of the E wave
2. the onset of the E wave to the onset of the A wave
3. the QRS complex to aortic valve opening

**Respuesta del original:** the end of LVOT flow to the onset of the E wave

**Explicación:** The aortic and mitral valves are both closed from aortic valve closure, at the end of LVOT flow, to mitral valve opening, at the onset of the E wave.

### 3. Which frame defines end-diastole on echocardiography?

1. The first frame after mitral valve closure
2. The peak of the T wave
3. Aortic valve opening

**Respuesta del original:** The first frame after mitral valve closure

**Explicación:** In Lang 2015, end-diastole is defined as the first frame after mitral valve closure or the frame with the largest LV dimension.
