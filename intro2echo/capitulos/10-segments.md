## Regional wall motion

The 17-segment model

In the standard model, the LV wall is divided into 17 segments. Each coronary artery usually supplies a specific set of segments, so the distribution of abnormal wall motion indicates the artery involved.

All 16 segments are scored 1 (normal), and the wall motion score index is 1.00[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14). The LAD, RCA and LCX buttons on the figure occlude the corresponding artery, and a tap on a segment advances it to the next score.

### Segment nomenclature

The segments are named counterclockwise from the anterior junction of the septum and the RV free wall: anteroseptal, inferoseptal, inferior, inferolateral, anterolateral and anterior[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14). The basal and mid levels each contain six segments, the apical level contains four, and the apical cap is segment 17[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14).

- Wall motion should be scored in 16 segments, and 17 segments should be used for perfusion or for comparison with other imaging modalities[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14).
- Each segment is scored as follows: 1, normal or hyperkinetic; 2, hypokinetic; 3, akinetic; 4, dyskinetic[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14). The 2025 reporting guideline adds a score of 5 for an aneurysmal segment[36](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-36). In the figure, a tap on a segment in the bull's-eye or in an apical view, or on its numbered button, advances that segment to the next score.
- The wall motion score index is the mean of the segment scores, and a value of 1.00 is normal[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14).

### Apical views and LV walls

The apical four-chamber view shows the inferoseptal and anterolateral walls, the two-chamber view the inferior and anterior walls, and the three-chamber view the inferolateral and anteroseptal walls[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14).

### Wall motion patterns of coronary occlusion

- **LAD.** LAD occlusion affects the anterior wall in the two-chamber view and the anteroseptal wall in the three-chamber view, each from base to apex; the apical septum in the four-chamber view; and the apex in all three views[41](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-41)[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14).
- **RCA.** RCA occlusion affects the inferior wall in the two-chamber view, from base to apex, and the basal and mid inferoseptum in the four-chamber view; the three-chamber view remains normal[41](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-41)[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14).
- **Circumflex.** Circumflex occlusion affects the anterolateral wall in the four-chamber view and the inferolateral wall in the three-chamber view; the two-chamber view remains normal[41](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-41)[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14).

### Coronary territories

In the standard map, the LAD supplies segments 1, 2, 7, 8, 13, 14 and 17; the right coronary artery supplies segments 3, 4, 9, 10 and 15; and the circumflex supplies segments 5, 6, 11, 12 and 16[41](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-41). Coronary supply varies between patients; in one CT study, 72% of patients had at least one segment supplied by a different artery[41](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-41). The map should therefore be regarded as a guide rather than a fixed rule.

Regional strain values are not recommended for clinical decision-making, because they vary between repeated examinations and between vendors[39](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-39).

### Key points

1. Wall motion is scored in 16 segments, and the wall motion score index is the mean of their scores.
2. Each apical view shows two opposite walls of the LV.
3. Abnormal motion of the anterior wall, the anteroseptum and the apex suggests LAD occlusion. Abnormal motion of the inferior wall and the basal and mid inferoseptum suggests RCA occlusion, and abnormal motion of the lateral walls suggests circumflex occlusion.

## Cuestionario del original

### 1. The anterior wall, the anteroseptal wall and the apex are akinetic. Which coronary artery is most likely occluded?

1. LAD
2. Right coronary
3. Circumflex

**Respuesta del original:** LAD

**Explicación:** In the standard map, the LAD supplies the anterior and anteroseptal segments and the apex.

### 2. Which apical view shows the inferior and anterior walls?

1. Four-chamber
2. Two-chamber
3. Three-chamber

**Respuesta del original:** Two-chamber

**Explicación:** In Lang 2015 (Figure 4), the two-chamber view shows segments 4, 10 and 15 of the inferior wall and segments 1, 7 and 13 of the anterior wall.

### 3. Six of 16 segments are scored 3, and the other ten are normal. What is the wall motion score index?

1. 1.38
2. 1.75
3. 2.00

**Respuesta del original:** 1.75

**Explicación:** The index is the mean score, (6 × 3 + 10 × 1) ÷ 16 = 28 ÷ 16 = 1.75.
