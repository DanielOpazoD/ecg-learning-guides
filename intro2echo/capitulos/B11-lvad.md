## Left ventricular assist devices

The HeartMate 3: the surveillance study, the speed ramp and complications

A continuous-flow LVAD takes blood from the LV apex through an inflow cannula and returns it to the ascending aorta through an outflow graft. Echocardiography shows how far the pump unloads the LV at each speed, and it detects the complications of the device.

**A working pump: the LV is unloaded.** At 5,300 rpm the LV internal diameter at end-diastole is 5.0 cm. The aortic valve opens in 1 of 3 beats, for 110 ms, and the septum is neutral[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). MR is mild (vena contracta 0.18 cm)[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29). TR is mild (vena contracta 0.20 cm)[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29), and the RV basal diameter is 3.6 cm (normal)[32](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-32). The inflow cannula's PW peak is 0.8 m/s, and 1.1 m/s with the artificial pulse[60](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-60). The outflow graft's CW peak is 1.3 m/s, below 2 m/s[60](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-60). The RVOT VTI is 16 cm, a total cardiac output of 4.3 L/min[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). The LV narrows by 0.15 cm per 100 rpm[62](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-62).

### The device

Echocardiography shows the inflow cannula and parts of the outflow graft, but not the impeller[60](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-60). The HeartMate 3 (HM3), now the only LVAD implanted, typically runs at 4,800 to 5,600 rpm[60](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-60).

Every 2 seconds the HM3 lowers its speed by 2,000 rpm for 0.15 s and then raises it by 4,000 rpm for 0.20 s. This artificial pulse makes a Doppler peak out of step with the heartbeat, not to be mistaken for intermittent obstruction or for opening of the aortic valve[60](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-60). The inflow usually peaks below 1.0 m/s, up to 1.2 m/s with the pulse; the outflow graft peaks below 2 m/s on CW Doppler, but the pulse can take it above 2 m/s[60](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-60).

### The surveillance study

A surveillance study should be considered about 2 weeks after implantation or before discharge, whichever comes first, then at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, and every 6 to 12 months after that[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61).

- **LV size.** The LV internal diameter at end-diastole (LVIDd) in the parasternal long axis is the most reproducible measure of the LV. By 3 months it can be expected to fall by at least 15% from its value before implantation[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61).
- **Aortic valve.** M-mode shows whether the valve opens[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). A valve that stays closed is linked to thrombus in the aortic root and to de novo AR[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61).
- **Septum.** At end-diastole, a leftward shift of the septum comes from a high RV end-diastolic pressure, a low LV preload or too high a speed; a rightward shift, from a high LV end-diastolic pressure: too low a speed, pump dysfunction, severe AR or a high afterload[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61).
- **Inflow cannula.** A well-placed cannula lies at or near the LV apex and points at the mitral valve, with laminar flow into it[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61).
- **Cardiac output.** When the aortic valve stays closed and there is no significant AR or PR, the total cardiac output from the RVOT VTI ( [chapter 07](https://intro2echo.com/pressure)) equals the LVAD's output[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61).

### The speed ramp

A ramp study records the echocardiogram at each step of speed: in the HM3 studies, from 4,600 rpm in steps of 100 rpm to 6,200 rpm[62](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-62), [63](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-63). Because a lower speed can open the aortic valve over a root thrombus and cause an embolism, the study starts after a check of the anticoagulation and of the baseline images for thrombus[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61).

As the speed of a working pump rises, the LV gets smaller, the aortic valve opens less often or for less time and the RVOT stroke volume rises[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). With the HM3, the LVEDD fell by 0.15 cm for each 100 rpm[62](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-62), and the RV volumes rose only at the higher speeds[64](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-64).

Below the minimum speed, the LVIDd rises from baseline, the septum may shift rightward, the MR may grow and the aortic valve may open more often or for longer. Above the maximum speed, the septum shifts leftward or impedes the flow into the cannula, and the TR may worsen[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). The optimal HM3 speed is 100 rpm below the maximum speed of optimal LV unloading[60](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-60), and the opening of the aortic valve is a secondary goal to the relief of heart failure[60](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-60).

A suction event, a partial occlusion of the inflow cannula by a segment of LV myocardium, usually comes from over-pumping, and a lower speed often corrects it; a low preload or a low afterload, as in hypovolemia or sepsis, can also cause it[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). Its signs are an LV smaller than about 3 cm, a leftward shift of the septum and worse TR, with or without ventricular ectopy and intermittent obstruction of the inflow[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). An inflow velocity above 1.5 m/s on CW Doppler points to an obstruction of the inflow, from suction or a malpositioned cannula[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61).

### RV failure

RV failure on LVAD support shows as a larger RV with lower systolic function, a dilated IVC, more TR, a lower RVOT stroke volume, and lower velocities in the cannula and the graft, below 0.5 m/s in severe failure[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). A septum bowed far to the left, with a small LV and a dilated, failing RV, means that the pump lacks preload, and the speed should be lowered[60](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-60). The RV basal diameter is graded as in [chapter 11](https://intro2echo.com/rv): normal below 4.1 cm, and severely dilated above 4.9 cm[32](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-32).

### Aortic regurgitation

De novo AR occurs in about 25% to 33% of patients by 12 months and affects the pump's performance, morbidity and mortality[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). The pump sends the regurgitant blood back to the aorta, a blind loop: the flow in the device is high, but the forward output, the RVOT cardiac output, falls and the LV grows[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). Higher speeds tend to increase the AR[60](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-60).

A vena contracta of 0.3 cm or more, or a jet width of more than 46% of the LVOT at a Nyquist limit of 50 to 60 cm/s, indicates at least moderate AR. The pressure half-time and the diastolic flow reversal in the aorta are not reliable on LVAD support, because the AR runs into systole, and with the valve shut it is continuous[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61) ( [chapter 13](https://intro2echo.com/ar)).

### Obstruction

A thrombus in the pump or an obstruction of the cannula or the graft reduces the pump's flow. Against the patient's baseline, the LV dilates again, the septum shifts rightward, the aortic valve opens more and the diastolic velocities in the cannula and the graft fall[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). When the speed is raised, the LVIDd, the opening of the aortic valve and the RVOT stroke volume do not change as expected[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61). Near an obstruction of the outflow graft the peak velocity is 2 m/s or more[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61); the figure samples the graft at the narrowing.

### Key points

1. With a working HM3, the LV gets smaller by 0.15 cm per 100 rpm[62](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-62). An LV below 3 cm with a leftward septum is a suction event[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61).
2. The Doppler peak of the HM3's artificial pulse, every 2 seconds, is neither an obstruction nor an opening of the aortic valve[60](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-60).
3. In de novo AR the pump flow is high while the RVOT output falls; a vena contracta of 0.3 cm or more indicates at least moderate AR[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61).
4. An LV, an aortic valve and an RVOT output that do not change with the speed point to an obstruction[61](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-61).
