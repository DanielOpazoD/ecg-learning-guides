TAPSE and S′ measure the motion of one point of the tricuspid annulus; FAC comes from the whole RV area in one plane. When TAPSE can underestimate RV function, as after cardiac surgery, an abnormal TAPSE should be reported only if adjunctive findings such as FAC or 3D RVEF support it.

### Biplane method of disks

The biplane method of disks (modified Simpson’s rule) is the recommended 2D method; 3D echocardiography should be used when it is available and image quality is good[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14). The LV is traced at end-diastole and end-systole, the frames that [chapter 05](https://intro2echo.com/cycle) defines, in the apical four- and two-chamber views. The software divides the LV into a stack of thin disks and sums their volumes. LV volumes should not be calculated from linear dimensions[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14).

The border is traced at the interface between the compacted myocardium and the cavity and is closed by a straight line across the mitral annulus. The LV length, L, is measured from the midpoint of that line to the most distant point of the border, and the longer L of the two views is used[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14). In the figure, each echocardiographic view displays its tracing, L and the 20 disks; the drawing below each view shows the model LV in the plane of that view.

Foreshortening of the apical views ( [chapter 04](https://intro2echo.com/windows)) leads to underestimation of the LV volumes[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14). An imaging depth set on the LV reduces the likelihood of foreshortening[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14). In a foreshortened view, the apex has a more rounded contour[4](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-4). In the figure, foreshortening reduces both volumes by the same fraction, so the EF changes little although both volumes are underestimated.

### Normal values

- Normal LVEF is 52–72% in men and 54–74% in women. LVEF is mildly abnormal at 41–51% in men and 41–53% in women, moderately abnormal at 30–40% and severely abnormal below 30%[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14).
- The EDV index is normal at 34–74 mL/m² in men and 29–61 mL/m² in women, and the ESV index at 11–31 mL/m² in men and 8–24 mL/m² in women[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14).
- The heart failure categories by LVEF are reduced (≤40%), mildly reduced (41–49%) and preserved (≥50%)[37](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-37).
- The valve chapters define LV dysfunction by other LVEF cut-offs: below 50% in aortic stenosis ( [chapter 12](https://intro2echo.com/as)), 55% or less in aortic regurgitation ( [chapter 13](https://intro2echo.com/ar)) and 60% or less in mitral regurgitation ( [chapter 15](https://intro2echo.com/mr))[38](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-38).
- Global longitudinal strain is normal when more negative than −18%, borderline from −16% to −18% and abnormal when less negative than −16%[39](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-39). GLS varies between vendors and software versions, so serial studies of one patient should use the same equipment and software[14](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-14).

The ASE reporting guideline describes global LV systolic function as normal, hyperdynamic or reduced, with a grade of the reduction[36](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-36). A single LVEF should be reported, together with the method of measurement[36](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-36). In serial 2D studies of stable patients, the EF varied by more than 10 percentage points; with non-contrast 3D echocardiography, it varied by about 6 percentage points[40](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-40).

RV systolic function (TAPSE, S′ and the fractional area change) is in [chapter 11](https://intro2echo.com/rv).

### Key points

1. LV volumes are measured by the biplane method of disks in the apical four- and two-chamber views, and foreshortening should be avoided.
2. The lower limit of normal LVEF is 52% in men and 54% in women.
3. Global longitudinal strain is abnormal when it is less negative than −16%[39](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-39).

## Cuestionario del original

### 1. The apical views are foreshortened by 15%. What is the effect on the measured EDV and ESV?

1. Both are underestimated
2. Both are overestimated
3. Only ESV changes

**Respuesta del original:** Both are underestimated

**Explicación:** A plane that misses the true apex shortens the traced LV length, so both volumes are underestimated.

### 2. An LVEF of 45% in a woman is classified as…

1. normal
2. mildly abnormal
3. moderately abnormal

**Respuesta del original:** mildly abnormal

**Explicación:** According to Lang 2015, an LVEF of 41–53% is mildly abnormal in women.

### 3. Global longitudinal strain is −15%. How is it classified?

1. Abnormal
2. Borderline
3. Normal

**Respuesta del original:** Abnormal

**Explicación:** GLS is normal when more negative than −18%, borderline from −16% to −18%, and abnormal when less negative than −16%.
