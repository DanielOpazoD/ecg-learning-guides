## Tricuspid transcatheter edge-to-edge repair

T-TEER, step by step: what TEE shows at each step

Tricuspid transcatheter edge-to-edge repair (T-TEER) joins the septal leaflet to the anterior or the posterior leaflet with a clip, brought from the femoral vein up the inferior vena cava into the right atrium. TEE guides each step of the procedure, with fluoroscopy, and TTE shows the result.

**Massive secondary TR.** The RV and the annulus are dilated, and the leaflets no longer meet: a central gap lets a wide jet into the RA. Vena contracta 1.6 cm, PISA EROA 0.65 cm², in the massive grade of the five-grade scale[119](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-119); the hepatic veins show systolic flow reversal[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29).

### The device

A steerable guide catheter enters the femoral vein, usually the right one, and runs up the inferior vena cava to the right atrium[108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108), [109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109). Its shaft is 25 Fr[110](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-110). The delivery system goes through it and carries the implant: two rigid arms, with a flexible gripper over each that drops on its own[109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109). The arms are 9 or 12 mm long, and 4 or 6 mm wide[109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109). The FDA approved a tricuspid TEER system on April 1, 2024, to improve quality of life and functional status in symptomatic severe TR despite optimal medical therapy, at intermediate or greater surgical risk, when a heart team judges the repair appropriate and expects TR of moderate or less[111](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-111).

### Who is treated

TRILUMINATE Pivotal randomly assigned patients with symptomatic severe TR to TEER with medical therapy or to medical therapy alone[112](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-112). In the first 350 patients, the hierarchical primary end point (death or tricuspid surgery, then heart failure hospitalization, then a gain of at least 15 points in the KCCQ score at 1 year) favored TEER, with a win ratio of 1.48[112](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-112). At 30 days, TR was moderate or less in 87.0% and 4.8%; at 1 year, the KCCQ score rose by 12.3 and 0.6 points[112](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-112). Death or surgery and heart failure hospitalization did not appear to differ at 1 year; quality of life carried the result[112](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-112), [113](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-113). In all 572 randomized patients, heart failure hospitalizations through 2 years were fewer with TEER (0.19 against 0.26 per patient-year), mortality was 17.9% and 17.1%, and 61.5% of the controls crossed over to a tricuspid intervention[114](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-114). In Tri.Fr (300 patients), a composite clinical score improved at 1 year in 74.1% with TEER and 40.6% with medical therapy alone[115](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-115).

The 2025 ESC/EACTS guidelines give transcatheter treatment a class IIa recommendation (level A) to improve quality of life and RV remodeling in high-risk patients with symptomatic severe TR despite optimal medical therapy, without severe RV dysfunction or pre-capillary pulmonary hypertension[84](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-84).

### The anatomy for TEER

TRILUMINATE excluded a coaptation defect over 2 cm, calcium in the grasping area, and leads that would prevent placement of the clip[113](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-113), and a valve area of 1.0 cm² or less or a mean gradient of 5 mmHg or more[110](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-110). A small gap and a central or anteroseptal jet predicted success in an early series[116](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-116); in bRIGHT, the 30-day reduction of TR was similar for gaps under 7 mm, 7–10 mm and over 10 mm[117](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-117). In selected patients with a transvalvular pacemaker or defibrillator lead, TR was moderate or less at 30 days as often as without one (88% and 87%)[118](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-118).

### The steps on TEE

1. **Guide in the RA.** The guide comes up the IVC. The bicaval view at about 90°, with biplane or live 3D, tracks it clear of the septum, the Eustachian valve, a Chiari network and leads[109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109).
2. **Steering.** The delivery system flexes down toward the annulus; the imager follows it from the bicaval to the four-chamber view, then to the RV inflow view and its biplane pair[109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109), [108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108).
3. **Alignment.** In the RA the arms open, and the clip turns until they lie square to the target line of coaptation[108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108): most accurately on the 3D en face view, or on the transgastric short axis[109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109). Without a focal defect, the anteroseptal line, usually the longest, comes first[109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109). The septal leaflet is grasped with the anterior or the posterior leaflet: a grasp of the anterior and posterior leaflets alone has not been shown to reduce TR[108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108). In the RA, each gripper is lowered and raised in turn to check its leaflet[109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109), [108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108).
4. **Grasping.** The clip is closed before it goes below the valve, to keep the leaflets clear of the grippers[108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108). There the arms open to 120–150°, the delivery catheter comes back, and the grippers drop onto the leaflets, slowly and in diastole[108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108). 2D views at 0–20° or about 140–160°, the biplane pair from the RV inflow-outflow view with its cursor through the device, or 3D multiplanar reconstruction guide the grasp[109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109). The systolic bounce of the grippers may not show on the low-pressure right side[109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109); when it shows, it confirms the capture[108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108). A tissue bridge on the transgastric short axis or in 3D confirms the insertion; the residual leaflet length, used on the mitral valve, is less reliable here, as the leaflets stretch[108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108).
5. **Assessment.** Before release, TEE checks the insertion again, the reduction of TR and the gradient[108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108). The 3D vena contracta area grades the residual TR best, as PISA tends to overestimate it with a clip in place[109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109). TVARC asks for a mean gradient under 5 mmHg[119](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-119). In the TriValve registry (top quartile 4.7 mmHg), a higher discharge gradient was not associated with death or heart failure hospitalization at 1 year[120](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-120).
6. **Release.** The clip is released, the delivery catheter comes out of it, and the system is withdrawn[108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108). In TRILUMINATE, a mean of 2.2 clips was implanted, two or three in about 85% of the patients[113](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-113); the figure shows the first.

The guidelines show the tricuspid valve en face with the septal leaflet at 6 o'clock, from either side[105](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-105), as the figure does. Many interventional imagers skip that turn: the septum is then on the right and the aorta near 5 o'clock[86](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-86).

### After the procedure

In TRILUMINATE, 98.3% of the patients treated were free of major adverse events at 30 days: cardiovascular death, new renal failure, endocarditis needing surgery, or surgery for a device problem[112](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-112), [110](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-110). Single-leaflet attachment was the main reason for a missed device success in the randomized device arm (11 of 18 patients)[113](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-113), and occurred in 5 of 72 (7%) in the first single-arm study[121](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-121). The mean gradient rose by 1.15 mmHg at 12 months with the clip and by 0.07 mmHg without it[113](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-113). At 2 years, 84% of the device group of all 572 patients had TR of moderate or less[114](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-114). In the bRIGHT registry (511 patients), TR was moderate or less in 77% at 30 days and 81% at 1 year[122](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-122), [123](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-123).

### Key points

1. The clip joins the septal leaflet to the anterior or the posterior leaflet, and the anteroseptal line usually comes first[108](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-108), [109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109).
2. Residual TR is graded by 3D vena contracta area rather than PISA, and a mean gradient under 5 mmHg is the target[109](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-109), [119](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-119).
3. In TRILUMINATE, the repair reduced TR and quality of life improved; death or tricuspid surgery and heart failure hospitalization did not appear to differ at 1 year[112](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-112).

## Cuestionario del original

### 1. Which pair of leaflets can a tricuspid clip grasp to reduce TR?

1. The anterior and posterior leaflets
2. The septal leaflet with the anterior or the posterior leaflet
3. Any two leaflets, with the same effect

**Respuesta del original:** The septal leaflet with the anterior or the posterior leaflet

**Explicación:** The septal leaflet is grasped with the anterior or the posterior leaflet. A grasp of the anterior and posterior leaflets alone has not been shown to reduce TR. Without a focal defect, the anteroseptal line, usually the longest, comes first.

### 2. The arms lie square to the anteroseptal line of coaptation. Which view shows their orientation most accurately?

1. The 3D en face view of the valve
2. The bicaval view
3. The parasternal long axis on TTE

**Respuesta del original:** The 3D en face view of the valve

**Explicación:** The 3D en face view, with multiplanar reconstruction to place the two 2D planes at the site of the grasp, shows the orientation most accurately. The transgastric short axis of the leaflet tips is the other check, with care for off-axis cuts.

### 3. After the grasp, the residual TR must be graded. Which method grades it best with a clip in place?

1. PISA and its EROA
2. The 3D vena contracta area
3. The color jet area in the RA

**Respuesta del original:** The 3D vena contracta area

**Explicación:** With a clip in place, PISA tends to overestimate the residual TR. The 3D vena contracta area grades it best.

### 4. In TRILUMINATE Pivotal, which outcome carried the primary end point at 1 year?

1. Fewer deaths
2. Fewer heart failure hospitalizations
3. A better quality of life (KCCQ)

**Respuesta del original:** A better quality of life (KCCQ)

**Explicación:** Death or tricuspid surgery and heart failure hospitalization did not appear to differ at 1 year. The KCCQ score rose by 12.3 points with TEER and by 0.6 points with medical therapy alone. In the hierarchy, a KCCQ gain of at least 15 points gave 21% of the wins to TEER and 7% to the control group.
