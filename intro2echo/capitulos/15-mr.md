## Mitral regurgitation

Left heart volume overload

In mitral regurgitation, part of the LV stroke volume flows back into the LA through the incompetent mitral valve during systole. The LV and LA dilate to accommodate the additional volume, and LV systolic dysfunction develops late in the course.

### Echocardiographic findings by grade and stage

1. A small central jet, normal leaflets, a vena contracta below 0.3 cm, absent flow convergence and A-wave-dominant mitral inflow indicate **mild** regurgitation[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29).
2. In **moderate** regurgitation, the LV and LA are normal in size or mildly dilated, and pulmonary vein flow is normal or shows systolic blunting[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29).
3. In **severe** regurgitation, the EROA is ≥0.40 cm², the regurgitant volume ≥60 mL, the regurgitant fraction ≥50% and the vena contracta ≥0.7 cm[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29). The LV and LA are dilated, the CW Doppler signal is dense and triangular, systolic flow in the pulmonary veins is absent or reversed, and the mitral E velocity exceeds 1.2 m/s[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29).
4. In the ACC/AHA classification, stage C1 is defined by an LVEF >60% and an LV end-systolic diameter <40 mm, and stage C2, with **LV dysfunction**, by an LVEF ≤60% or an LV end-systolic diameter ≥40 mm[38](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-38).

### Integrated grading

The ASE algorithm first counts the specific criteria. Four or more specific criteria for mild MR define mild regurgitation, and four or more for severe MR define severe regurgitation[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29). Otherwise, the EROA, the regurgitant volume and the regurgitant fraction assign a grade from I to IV. Grade I is mild, grades II and III are moderate, and grade IV is severe[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29). Grade III with three specific criteria for severe MR, or with an elliptical orifice, also indicates severe regurgitation[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29). If the findings are inconsistent, the grade is indeterminate, and TEE or CMR can quantify the regurgitation[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29). The algorithm gives no rule for discordant quantitative measures; the diagram uses the middle grade of the three.

### PISA method

flow = 2π r² × Va  ·  EROA = flow ÷ peak velocity  ·  RVol = EROA × VTI

Blood accelerates as it converges on the regurgitant orifice; at the aliasing velocity (Va), the color changes, and the isovelocity surface at Va is a hemisphere of radius r[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29). The color baseline should be shifted toward the jet to lower Va to about 30 to 40 cm/s, and the radius should be measured at the time of the peak jet velocity[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29). At this setting, a radius of 1.0 cm or more is specific for severe MR[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29). The method applies the principle of [chapter 07](https://intro2echo.com/pressure): the flow rate through a surface equals its area × the velocity.

The figure applies the method in four steps:

1. The color scale is set so that the flow aliases at 32 cm/s. In a study, the view is zoomed on the mitral valve, and the color baseline is shifted toward the jet.
2. The radius r runs from the orifice to the first aliasing boundary. The flow rate is 2πr² × 32 cm/s.
3. The flow rate divided by the peak MR velocity of 5.0 m/s gives the EROA.
4. The EROA multiplied by the VTI of the jet gives the regurgitant volume. A jet through all of systole has a VTI of 150 cm.

For example, an EROA of 0.40 cm² at a peak velocity of 5.0 m/s corresponds to a flow rate of 200 mL/s. At 32 cm/s, this flow rate gives a radius of 1.00 cm, and the regurgitant volume is 60 mL. All three values reach the thresholds for severe MR[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29). At the same velocity, an EROA of 0.10 cm² gives a radius of 0.50 cm, larger than the 0.3 cm of a small flow convergence in mild MR[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29).

- The radius is squared in the flow equation; errors of 10 to 25% are common, even among experts[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29).
- An elliptical orifice, which is common in secondary MR, causes PISA to underestimate the flow[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29). In secondary MR, an EROA ≥0.30 cm² by 2D PISA may still indicate severe regurgitation[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29); the EACVI accepts an EROA ≥0.30 cm² or a regurgitant volume ≥45 mL as severe[65](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-65).
- When regurgitation occupies only part of systole, the regurgitant volume should be used, because a measurement from a single frame overestimates the EROA[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29).

### Pitfalls

- A normal LA size generally excludes severe chronic MR, whereas a dilated LA alone does not establish it[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29).
- Color jet area is useful to exclude MR but is unreliable for grading its severity; for the assessment of regurgitant jets, the color scale should be set to 50 to 70 cm/s[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29) ( [chapter 03](https://intro2echo.com/shift)). The figure shows the flow convergence at an aliasing velocity of 32 cm/s. Its jet area is the value at a color scale of 50 to 70 cm/s.
- Systolic flow reversal in the pulmonary veins is specific for severe regurgitation but is not sensitive[29](https://intro2echo.com/#ref-29).

### Key points

1. Four or more specific criteria for mild or for severe MR define the grade; otherwise, the EROA, the regurgitant volume and the regurgitant fraction decide.
2. Mitral regurgitation is severe when the EROA is ≥0.40 cm², the regurgitant volume is ≥60 mL and the regurgitant fraction is ≥50%.
3. A mitral E velocity >1.2 m/s and systolic flow reversal in the pulmonary veins support a severe grade.
4. An LVEF ≤60% or an LV end-systolic diameter ≥40 mm indicates ACC/AHA stage C2.

## Cuestionario del original

### 1. At a constant regurgitant flow, the aliasing velocity is lowered from 40 to 30 cm/s. How do the PISA radius and the EROA change?

1. The radius increases; the EROA does not change
2. The radius decreases
3. The radius increases; the EROA increases

**Respuesta del original:** The radius increases; the EROA does not change

**Explicación:** The flow, 2πr² × Va, is constant, so r² increases as Va decreases; the EROA, flow ÷ peak velocity, does not change.

### 2. In mitral regurgitation, the EROA is 0.45 cm² and the regurgitant volume is 68 mL. What is the grade?

1. Moderate
2. Severe
3. Mild

**Respuesta del original:** Severe

**Explicación:** An EROA ≥0.40 cm² and a regurgitant volume ≥60 mL meet the criteria for severe regurgitation.

### 3. A patient with severe primary MR has no symptoms, an LVEF of 58% and an LV end-systolic diameter of 36 mm. What is the ACC/AHA stage?

1. C1
2. C2
3. D

**Respuesta del original:** C2

**Explicación:** Stage C2 is defined by an LVEF ≤60% or an LV end-systolic diameter ≥40 mm. An LVEF of 58% alone is sufficient.
